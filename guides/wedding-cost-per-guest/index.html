Wedding Cost Per Guest

The Knot's national wedding cost per guest is $292. The bill you avoid by cutting a name is smaller.

The $292 figure is the $34,200 average divided across 117 guests. It is published as a national rate. State and city pages that show a per-guest amount say when it is simply their total divided by 117 or by the guest count you picked.

Food is priced per guest in The Knot's study, at $80. Drinks, cake, rentals, favors, and invitations are treated as growing with the headcount. The venue, photographer, music, and clothes stay put. That is why the wedding cost per guest calculator shows a smaller savings when you cut the list.

Common questions

What is the wedding cost per guest?

The Knot's 2026 national figure is $292. Divide a local total by the guest count for a local all-in figure, and read the label so you know whether that rate was published or calculated.

How much does cutting 10 guests save?

Less than 10 times the all-in cost per guest. The meal and a few other lines shrink. The venue and the photographer do not. The calculator shows the smaller number for your place.
