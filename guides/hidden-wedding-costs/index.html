Hidden Wedding Costs

The number on a menu is often the number before the service charge and the tax.

Zola's spend survey put unexpected costs at $3,314 on average. 57% of couples faced a mandatory venue service fee. A service charge commonly runs 18–22%. Zola's venue guide mentions a top end of 25%.

Plus-plus means the menu price still needs the service charge and the tax. A service charge is not automatically a tip. Read the contract before you add gratuity on top. The checker on the cost per guest page adds the percents you type. It does not guess a state tax rate.

Zola also says weekday or winter dates can run 20–30% less at many vendors. That is a discount range, not a promise. The Knot study says 41% of couples went over budget. Zola's survey says 69% did. Different studies, both on the record.

About this page and common questions

Common questions

What are hidden wedding costs?

Costs that show up after the first quote. Zola's survey averaged them at $3,314. A common one is a venue service charge of 18–22%.

What does plus-plus mean on a wedding menu?

The printed price still needs a service charge and sales tax. Ask whether gratuity is already inside the service charge.

Do off-peak weddings cost less?

Zola says many vendors discount weekday or winter weddings by about 20–30%. Confirm it in the contract. The main total on the budget calculator stays put until you edit it.
