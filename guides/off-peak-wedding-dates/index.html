    The Knot publishes different numbers for the season of the wedding: $33,200 for January–March, $34,300 for April–June, $35,400 for July–September, and $33,200 for October–December, against $34,200 overall.     Ask the venue and the caterer whether the date you want is in their off-peak rate. The budget calculator can show the same range beside a category split.

    About this page and common questions

    Common questions

    Do off-peak weddings cost less?

    Zola says many vendors discount weekday or winter weddings by about 20–30%. That is a vendor discount range, not a new national average. The planning total on the card is not reduced until a contract says so.

    Is that the same as The Knot's season averages?

    No. The Knot's national season averages are winter $33,200, spring $34,300, summer $35,400, and fall $33,200, against $34,200 overall. Those stay separate from Zola's 20–30% discount.

    Every figure is cited on the sources page.
