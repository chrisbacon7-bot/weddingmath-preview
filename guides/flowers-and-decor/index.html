    What this number means

    The Knot prints flowers and lighting as two averages. Zola groups flowers and decor into one average, which is not mixed into this card.

    2026 studies · reviewed Oct 2026 Sourced: The Knot Real Weddings Study (updated July 28, 2026) · Zola (March 6, 2026) · Sources

    Flowers average $2,800 in The Knot's study. Lighting and decor average $1,900. Zola groups flowers and decor at $6,345, with a range of $5,100–$7,600. That Zola figure is not added to the Knot lines.

    A shop list can show a product whose page we opened, with the price and the date. That price is not a substitute for the $2,800 flower average. The DIY guide compares a supply cost you type with the hired lines.
