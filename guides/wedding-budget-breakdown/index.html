Wedding Budget Breakdown

A wedding budget breakdown is a total first, then the lines that add up to it.

The Knot's venue average is $12,900. Catering averages $80 per guest. The full vendor list is on the sources page. Those averages are among couples who hired that vendor. Added raw, they come to more than the $34,200 wedding average, because not every couple hires every vendor.

The wedding budget calculator keeps the shape of those averages and scales the lines so they add up to your total. Priorities move money between lines. The total stays put. Zola publishes its own category averages, including a venue average of $8,573. Those stay out of the Knot-shaped split.

Rehearsal dinner ($2,800 in The Knot's study), rings, and the honeymoon are usually outside that total.

Common questions

What is a typical wedding budget breakdown?

The lines follow The Knot's vendor averages and are scaled to the local total or a total you type. The national Knot average is $34,200. The venue average, among couples who hired a venue, is $12,900.

Why don't the category averages add up to the wedding average?

Each category average is for couples who paid that vendor. Most couples skip some vendors. The calculator scales the lines so they add up to $34,200 nationally, or to the local total.

Can we put more money toward one part?

Yes. Pick up to three priorities on the wedding budget calculator. They increase those lines and reduce the others. The total does not change.

Every figure is cited on the sources page.
