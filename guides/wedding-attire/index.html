    About this page and common questions

    Common questions

    How much is a wedding dress?

    The Knot's dress average is $2,100. Zola's dress average is $2,000, and Zola also publishes alterations at $550 and a tuxedo rental at $225. The card is the Knot dress plus hair and makeup.

    Are hair and makeup for the whole party?

    The Knot figures used here are $150 for hair and $150 for makeup, each described as one person. They are not multiplied by a bridal-party size, because that multiplier is not in the study.

    Every figure is cited on the sources page.
