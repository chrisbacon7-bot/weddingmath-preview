About this page and common questions

What these wedding planning guides cover

These guides explain the published figures. The calculators do the local math. State pages are the local guides: each one has that state's Knot average, Zola range, city studies, and estimated metros.

Common questions

Where are the local wedding cost guides?

On the state pages under Wedding cost. Each state uses its own published figures. A metro without a city study is labeled estimated.

Are the guides the same as the calculators?

The guides explain the studies. The wedding cost calculator, budget calculator, affordability tool, and cost-per-guest calculator apply those studies to a place and a guest count.

Every figure is cited on the sources page.
