    About this page and common questions

    Common questions

    Why is a band more than a DJ?

    The Knot's national live-band average is $4,500 and the reception-DJ average is $1,800. The band figure here is that ratio times your local music line. It is not a local band survey.

    What does a playlist cost?

    There is no published playlist average in our sources. The music line drops out until you type a cost.
