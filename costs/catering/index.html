    About this page and common questions

    Common questions

    Do plated, buffet, family style, stations, and food trucks have different prices here?

    No published study in our sources separates those styles. The first number is The Knot's food line for your place and guest count. Type a caterer's price if you have one.

    Is the service charge included?

    Not until you type a percent. Zola cites 18–22% on many quotes. That range is a hint, not a default added to the total.
