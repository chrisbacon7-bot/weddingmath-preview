About this page and common questions

What a honeymoon budget covers

The Knot's 2026 average honeymoon is $5,500. Zola's is $5,300. 71% of couples in The Knot's study took a honeymoon. Those averages sit on top of the wedding. They are outside the ceremony-and-reception total.

Common questions

How much does a honeymoon cost?

The Knot's 2026 average is $5,500. Zola's 2026 average is $5,300. 71% of couples in The Knot's study took one. The two studies stay separate.

Is the honeymoon inside the average wedding cost?

No. The Knot wedding average of $34,200 and the honeymoon average of $5,500 are separate figures. Plan the trip on top of the wedding.

Where do we price the actual trip?

VacationMath, linked from this page, prices flights, lodging, and daily spending for places such as Maui, plus all-inclusive resorts, cruises, and how to fund the trip.
