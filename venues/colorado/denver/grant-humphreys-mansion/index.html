At 100 guests, Saturday. History Colorado wedding brochure. Ceremony-and-reception rates start at these Saturday figures: $2,800 in January–April, $3,000 in November, and $4,000 in May–October and December. The low and high are that Saturday span. Friday in May–October starts at $3,100 and Sunday at $2,900. Monday–Thursday in that season starts at $1,800. Ceremony-only Saturdays start at $850 to $1,000 depending on the month. Holiday and extra-hour fees are not added into the range. The brochure says prices can change and to ask for the current rate.

What's included

  • The whole mansion, indoor and outdoor, for a ceremony and reception or a reception only
  • A flexible 7-hour base that includes two hours of setup and one hour of clean-up
  • Tables, chairs, a rehearsal hour, getting-ready rooms, basic AV, piano, elevator and lift for accessibility, and limited parking

Not included

  • Holiday add-ons: $500 on Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, and Christmas Eve, and $1,000 on New Year's Eve
  • Extra hours at $200 per hour
  • Day-of coordination, priced separately from $750 to $1,850
