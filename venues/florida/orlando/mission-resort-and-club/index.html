What's included

  • 500-acre resort that markets Central Florida weddings, with guest suites
  • Basic ceremony package includes chairs, a hydration station, and an indoor weather backup
  • Reception package includes passed hors d'oeuvres, dinner, a 4-hour bar, cake cutting, and two nights in a suite

Not included

  • Food and beverage are exclusive to the resort; a day-of planner is required and is not the catering manager
  • Wedding package pricing is based on the menu, and no Saturday total is printed
