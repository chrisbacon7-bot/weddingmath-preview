At 100 guests, Saturday. City of Winter Park outdoor wedding list: Kraft Azalea Garden is $600 for a two-hour slot between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m., plus a $500 deposit. Groups of 20 or fewer. Tables and chairs are not permitted. The fee list prints 1305 Alabama Drive. The park's own profile page prints 1365 Alabama Drive and says the Exedra holds up to 20 guests. The deposit is not included. The page does not print a different Saturday price, so both day columns use $600.
