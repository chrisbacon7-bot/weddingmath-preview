All-in breakdown

All-in $12,204 for 100 guests on Saturday, peak season. Published. Refundable deposits are not in the total. Prices change. Confirm with the venue before booking.

Needs a human look: This all-in total uses the guide's starting catering price of $65 per person. Higher menus are not priced. Bar packages start at $36 per person and are not included. The ceremony line is $6 per guest and does not enforce the $600 minimum under 100 guests. The catering pages say prices are valid for events from June 1 through December 31, 2026, while the venue chart calls September through May peak season.

What's included

  • Saturday peak-season rental starting at $2,500, September through May, before the 22% service charge and 6.5% tax
  • Ceremony fee of $6 per person, with a $600 minimum
  • The lodge page gives 1300 S. Denning Drive and says the garden accommodates events with indoor and outdoor ceremony options

Not included

  • The garden's own rental inquiry, which does not print a fee. That is a separate card for Mead Botanical Garden
  • Drink packages starting at $36 per person
  • Off-peak and weekday rates. A Friday example of $2,500 in the guide is one couple's booking, not a printed Friday rate for every date

Ask on the tour

  2. Which catering package applies above the $65 start?
  3. Is 175 the seated dinner count?
