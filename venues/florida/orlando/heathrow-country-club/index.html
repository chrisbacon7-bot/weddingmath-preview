What's included

  • Reception packages include five hours, a champagne or sparkling-cider toast, tables, linens, and chiavari chairs in the ballroom
  • The Garden Gazebo ceremony area is listed for up to 200 guests, with a one-hour rehearsal and a bridal suite

Not included

  • A package price
  • Guest rooms. The page says out-of-town guests use nearby hotels
  • The Legacy Club at Alaqua Lakes, which the same page describes for a rehearsal dinner or welcome reception rather than as this ceremony site

Ask on the tour

  1. What is the Saturday reception package price, and what is the separate ceremony fee?
  2. How many guests can sit in the Grand Ballroom?
  3. Is the Legacy Clubhouse a second wedding site or only a rehearsal location?
