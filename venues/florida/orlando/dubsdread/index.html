What's included

  • Saturday peak-season rental starting at $3,000 for the historic ballroom, September through May, before the 22% service charge and 6.5% tax
  • Ceremony fee of $6 per person, with a $600 minimum
  • Plated and buffet catering starting at $65 per person. Tables, chairs, china, glassware, and standard linens

Not included

  • Drink packages, which start at $36 per person, and dessert packages, which start at $15 per person
  • Higher catering packages. The guide says they exist and does not print those prices
  • Off-peak dates. The guide says to ask about value pricing and does not print those rates
  • A weekday price. The Saturday column is the peak-season starting rental only

Ask on the tour

  1. Is the Saturday we want inside the September–May peak rate, or the unpriced off-peak rate?
  2. Which catering package applies above the $65 starting plated or buffet?
  3. Does a reception without a ceremony drop the $6 per person fee?
  4. The catering pages say prices are valid June 1 through December 31, 2026. Which guide covers a date after that?
