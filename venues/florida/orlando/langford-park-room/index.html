Langford Park room

Historic

1808 East Central Boulevard, Orlando, FL 32803

Pricing not published

Pricing not published, ask the venue

At 100 guests, Saturday. Orlando parks page for Mayor Carl T. Langford Park, checked October 3, 2026. The neighborhood center is the room rental on the same page, which says it can be reserved for weddings. The room holds 35 to 50 people. The rate is $45 an hour, including setup and take-down, plus $15 an hour for a staff person. No minimum number of hours is printed, so the hourly rate is not turned into a flat total. Kitchen rental is $25 an hour if food is served. A $100 refundable deposit is extra. Address: 1808 East Central Boulevard, Orlando, FL 32803. Pricing not published as a flat site fee, ask the city.
