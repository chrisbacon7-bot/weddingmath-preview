$650–$3,575

At 100 guests, Saturday. From the Art & History Museums wedding PDF on artandhistory.org. Saturday figures on that sheet run from $650 for a morning elopement (up to 15 guests, 9–11am) to $3,575. The PDF is set in columns, so this page shows the published Saturday span instead of pinning one column to the wrong package. Peak season is listed as October–May and off-season as June–September. Tax is extra.

What's included

  • The PDF lists ceremony sites (chapel, courtyard, gardens) and reception rooms, including the Germaine Marvel Building
  • A $500 refundable security deposit is listed apart from the rental
  • Maitland residents get 5% off

Not included

  • Sales tax. The PDF says prices do not include it
  • Tables and chairs in the historic outdoor rooms
  • Catering. Food prices are not in the wedding PDF

Ask on the tour

  1. Which package is the $3,575 Saturday, and which is the $650 morning elopement?
  2. Is Germaine Marvel available in June–September? The PDF says that room is not
  3. Do we have to bring tables for the courtyard?
  4. Is a coordinator required?
