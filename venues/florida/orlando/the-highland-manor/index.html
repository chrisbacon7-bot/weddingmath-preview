All-in estimate

$14,774

At 100 guests, Saturday. Events by Dubsdread 2026 Q2 pricing guide, checked October 3, 2026. The Highland Manor is the Apopka column: Saturday peak-season rental starting at $4,500, September through May, ceremony $6 per person with a $600 minimum, before 22% service charge and 6.5% tax. Catering starts at $65 per person for plated or buffet. The manor's own page says ceremonies and receptions run about five hours, or four hours for a reception only, and that a wedding can be for 20 to 350 guests. The pricing guide's accommodations line says 20 to 250 at The Highland Manor and Historic Dubsdread. This card uses 350 from the manor page and flags the conflict. No street address was printed. A Sunday example of $3,500 in the guide is one couple's reception, not the Saturday rate, so it is not the off-day price.

All-in breakdown

All-in $14,774 for 100 guests on Saturday, peak season. Published. Refundable deposits are not in the total. Prices change. Confirm with the venue before booking.

Needs a human look: This all-in total uses the guide's starting catering price of $65 per person. Higher menus are not priced. Bar packages start at $36 per person and are not included. The catering pages say prices are valid for events from June 1 through December 31, 2026, while the venue chart calls September through May peak season.

What's included

  • Saturday peak-season rental starting at $4,500, September through May, before the 22% service charge and 6.5% tax
  • Ceremony fee of $6 per person, with a $600 minimum
  • The manor page describes the Crystal Ballroom, Oak Room, Glass Verandah, and Oak Bar, and says a wedding can be for up to 350 guests

Not included

  • A street address. The manor page does not print one. The company footer at 549 West Par Street is Historic Dubsdread, not this estate
  • Drink packages starting at $36 per person, and higher catering packages that are not priced
  • Off-peak and weekday rates. They are not printed
