At 100 guests, Saturday. Orlando Museum of Art weddings page, checked October 3, 2026. The museum hosts weddings at 2416 N. Mills Ave., Orlando, FL 32803. The page lists a full-museum rental up to 200 seated guests, the rotunda up to 100, the Grand Gallery up to 100 seated, the auditorium at 250 theater seats, and Sculpture Plaza up to 150. Standard packages on the page include five event hours. The page links a rental kit whose filename is OMA-WEDDING-RENTAL-KIT_21-22.pdf. That kit's wedding-package table lists a full-museum rental at $5,700, other space combinations at $4,000, $4,300, and $5,500, and a Grand Gallery ceremony at $1,600, and it says rental fees plus sales tax and a service fee are subject to change. The webpage capacities do not match the kit, so those dollars are not entered as the current site fee. Pricing not published, ask the venue.

What's included

  • The weddings page describes ceremonies and receptions in the galleries, rotunda, auditorium, and Sculpture Plaza
  • ADA entrances, restrooms, and paths. Tables, chairs, and a kitchen for the caterer are listed as included amenities

Not included

  • A current site fee on the webpage itself. The linked rental kit is the 2021–22 file, and the page says fees are in that kit
  • Catering. The kitchen is described as support for vendors

Ask on the tour

  1. Are the kit's $4,000 to $5,700 wedding packages still the current Saturday rates?
  2. Does the five-hour package include the reception, or only the 1.5-hour ceremony and cocktail line printed under each package?
  3. Is the Truist Auditorium's 250 seats a ceremony count only?
