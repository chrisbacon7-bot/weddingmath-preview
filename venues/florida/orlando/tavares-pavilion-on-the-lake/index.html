At 100 guests, Saturday. Tavares Pavilion on the Lake page, checked October 3, 2026. Whether a wedding, family gathering, corporate function, or a special celebration, the Tavares Pavilion has been uniquely designed to make it memorable. Saturday is the city's preferred entire-facility rate, any 8-hour block, $6,000, from the rates page at tavarespavilion.com/159/Rates. Monday–Wednesday evening full-building rental (6 p.m.–11 p.m.) is $1,750. Thursday evening entire facility is $2,200. Friday evening (5 p.m.–11 p.m.) and Sunday (any 6-hour block) are $3,500. New Year's Eve 8-hour block is $8,000. Weekday hourly ballroom rates have a 2-hour minimum and are a different product, not multiplied into these block fees. Capacity 598 is the city's printed max occupancy. Pricing not published, ask the venue.
