What's included

  • 8.5 hours on site, including the gardens
  • 3 hours to set up, 4.5 hours for the event, and 1 hour to break down
  • Two hospitality suites

Not included

  • Food and drink. Arthur's Catering is the exclusive caterer. Their wedding menu pages list dishes and do not print a per-person price. Marketplace starting prices are not used
  • The 6.5% tax the venue says to add
  • Extra hours, which are by request

Ask on the tour

  1. Are we booking October–April or the summer rate?
  2. What is Arthur's food minimum for our guest count?
  3. Is the courtyard tent included if it rains?
  4. The house holds 80 indoors and 120 outdoors. Which count are we planning?
