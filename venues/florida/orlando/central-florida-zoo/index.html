At 100 guests, Saturday. Central Florida Zoo venue-rental page, checked October 3, 2026. The weddings section says the zoo in Sanford is a setting for ceremonies and receptions, indoors and outdoors. Address on the page: 3755 W. Seminole Blvd., Sanford, FL 32771. The weddings section links a guide dated 2023. That guide lists a Paradise Plus package at $4,500 plus tax on Friday, Saturday, or Sunday and $4,000 plus tax Monday–Thursday for ceremony and reception, and a Paradise reception-only package at $3,800 plus tax. A separate Private Rental Guide dated 2026 lists 4-hour room rates, including full Safari Hall at $1,125, and does not use the word wedding. Those two sheets are not the same product, so neither figure is entered as the Saturday site fee. Pricing not published, ask the venue. The 2026 guide says outdoor events are rain or shine and extra indoor space is not promised.
