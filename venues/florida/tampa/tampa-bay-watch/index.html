What's included

  • Nine hours for ceremony and reception, including setup and teardown. One wedding a day
  • Indoor and outdoor areas, a 1,900-square-foot veranda, a prep room, tables and chairs, electricity, and parking
  • The ceremony site only when the reception is also on the property

Not included

  • The rental dollar amount. The page says pricing depends on the event and date
  • A $1,000 non-refundable deposit, and a card on file for damage
  • Arch, altar tables, and setup of the furniture, which the renter does
  • Event insurance if alcohol is served: $1,000,000, naming Tampa Bay Watch

Ask on the tour

  1. What is the Saturday facility fee, and what is Friday or Sunday?
  2. How many ceremony chairs are in the inventory?
  3. If it rains, which indoor room is the backup, and does it hold 150?
