All-in estimate

$8,912–$14,163

At 100 guests, Saturday. Cuban Club weddings page, checked October 3, 2026. The “save” amounts next to each package are not used as the fee.

What's included

  • Choose-your-own caterer, decorator, florist, and entertainment. Private rooms for the wedding party
  • Friday and Saturday packages: Diamond $5,500 (ballroom, theatre ceremony, lobby cocktail hour, both salons), Ruby $4,100, Sapphire $3,700, Emerald $2,850 (Cantina reception), and Pearl $2,450
  • Open-bar prices printed per person for four hours: Premium $25 and Standard $20. Soda and water $5

Not included

  • Catering
  • A beer-and-wine open-bar price. That line on the page did not render as a clean dollar amount, so it is not stored
  • A Monday-through-Thursday package. The listed packages say Friday and Saturday

Ask on the tour

  1. Do the package prices include tax, and is there a security or insurance fee?
  2. What is the beer-and-wine open-bar price per person?
  3. What is the seated capacity of the ballroom and of the Cantina?
