All-in estimate

$8,686–$19,379

At 100 guests, Saturday. The museum's October 15, 2025 rental brochure lists several Saturday prices, so this is the published Saturday span rather than one room. Non-peak (July 1–September 30) Saturday runs from $2,250 for one garden to $9,250 for Conservatory, Marly Room, and Membership Garden. Peak (October 1–June 30) Saturday runs from $7,250 for the Marly Room with both gardens to $10,750 for Conservatory, terrace, Marly, and Membership Garden. Conservatory with terrace is $7,500 peak Saturday and $6,500 that Friday or Sunday. Food is not included. The brochure does not state a tax percent.

What's included

  • Five-hour rental, security, and the Bayview room for three hours before the event
  • A set of rounds, high-tops, and banquet tables. Marly Room rentals include 180 chairs; Conservatory and garden chairs are extra
  • Straub Park ceremonies require one of the museum rental options

Not included

  • Catering. Constellation Culinary Group is the exclusive caterer and quotes food separately
  • Gallery access at $500 per hour and a docent tour at $100
  • Chairs for the Conservatory, gardens, and Straub Park

Ask on the tour

  1. Is the date in peak season, October 1–June 30, or non-peak, July 1–September 30?
  2. Which rooms are in the rental: Conservatory, Marly, gardens, or a combination?
  3. What is Constellation's food-and-bar quote, and is sales tax added to the rental?
