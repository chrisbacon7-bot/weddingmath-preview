What's included

  • A county wedding application for the gardens, including the Wedding Garden with a white gazebo
  • Admission to the gardens is free. Hours are 7 a.m. to sunset

Not included

  • A dollar rental. The gardens page links to the wedding application and does not print a fee
  • Chairs, catering, and a backup room
