What's included

  • Gardens and Garden Room package: ceremony on the North Lawn, cocktail hour in the Oak Pavilion, reception in the Garden Room, up to 176 guests
  • Rehearsal of up to one hour, parking, and a dressing room, as the FAQ describes the rental

Not included

  • The 3% sales tax, which is added on the card
  • A $250 refundable damage deposit
  • Catering. Approved caterers bill a 15% service charge that is not a sales tax and is not in this number
  • Tent packages, which are priced separately

Ask on the tour

  1. Is the Oak Pavilion package, at $8,250 for up to 72 guests, a better fit?
  2. Do we need the tent? White-top is $23,000 and clear-top is $30,000 for 2027–2028
  3. Alcohol must be bought through the Gardens. What is the bar minimum?

Venue website
