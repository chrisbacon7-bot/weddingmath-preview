What's included

  • Exclusive use for a ceremony, cocktails, and a four-hour reception, plus two hours of photography and the upstairs suite before the ceremony
  • Tram from the parking lot, a ceremony host, free parking, five hours of setup, and 90 minutes of cleanup
  • The rate table prints November–April, a Friday/Sunday November–April column, and May–October

Not included

  • Catering. The page titles the table property rental only
  • A $2,200 veranda tent from October through April
  • A $1,000 refundable deposit, a $100 couple's membership, and a police detail of $350 up to 150 guests or $600 above that
  • Overnight use of the suite, which is an extra fee

Ask on the tour

  1. Our guest count falls in which row, and is the date May–October or November–April?
  2. The Friday/Sunday column is already 15% off. Saturday uses the first column in season. Is that our cell?
  3. Who may cater, and is the bar through that caterer?
