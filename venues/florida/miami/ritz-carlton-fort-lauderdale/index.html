What's included

  • Grand Ballroom with Hungarian crystal chandeliers. One section says up to 400 guests and another says the 8,500-square-foot ballroom accommodates weddings of up to 380
  • Astor Ballroom, 2,838 square feet, weddings of up to 100 guests, with an ocean-view foyer and terrace
  • Seventh-floor pool deck overlooking the beach
  • More than 24,000 square feet of indoor and outdoor event space
  • Guest-room blocks. A discount is described for 10 or more rooms

Not included

  • A beach ceremony. The FAQ says the hotel does not offer beach ceremonies
  • An on-site florist
  • A printed package price. The FAQ says a food-and-beverage minimum applies and does not print it
  • Chiavari chairs, specialty linens, and specialty base plates, which can be extra

Ask on the tour

  1. Is the occupancy maximum 400 or 380 for a Saturday in the Grand Ballroom?
  2. What is the Saturday food-and-beverage minimum?
  3. Can we book a ceremony without a reception?
  4. The guarantee is due 72 working hours ahead. Is the minimum still due if the count drops?
