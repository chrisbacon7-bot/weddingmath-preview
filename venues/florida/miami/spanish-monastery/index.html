The Ancient Spanish Monastery

HistoricGarden

Published venue rental

$12,000

At 100 guests, Saturday. From the monastery's wedding PDF, the Saturday wedding-with-reception package in the Loggia is $12,000 for up to 250 people. Friday or Sunday in the Loggia is $11,000. Monday–Thursday in the Loggia is $9,000. Other rooms are less, down to $6,000 on a weekday in the Chapter House. The PDF does not add sales tax, so tax is not added here. Food is not included. What's included

  • The quoted package is a Saturday wedding and reception in the Loggia, up to 250 people, for four hours
  • An officiant and the monastery's pianist or organist
  • A security guard at the gate, a ceremony coordinator, and the changing room

Not included

  • Catering, rentals, and decor. The PDF says the monastery provides the venue only
  • The bridal suite upgrade, listed at $250
  • Summer discounts and the smaller rooms, which cost less than the Loggia

Ask on the tour

  1. Which of the two wedding PDFs on the site is the current one? A second file's ceremony-only line did not extract cleanly.
  2. The Chapter House package is $9,000 on Saturday. Is that enough room?
  3. What does an approved caterer cost on top of the $12,000?
  4. June–September takes $400 off this package. Does our date qualify?
