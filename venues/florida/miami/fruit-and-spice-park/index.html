Published Saturday range

$300–$3,100

At 100 guests, Saturday. Miami-Dade Fruit & Spice Park facility-rentals page, checked October 3, 2026. Three products share this range. A garden ceremony is $300 for two hours between 10 a.m. and 3 p.m., up to 50 guests. The Bauer-Neill-Mitchell House and the Citrus House are also $300. The Tropical daytime rental, 10 a.m.–4 p.m., is $2,100 for up to 125 guests, with a maximum of 250 and a $4-per-person catering fee. The Paradise evening rental, 5 p.m.–midnight, is $3,100 for up to 125 guests; extra guests are $15 per person up to 250, plus a $4-per-person catering fee. The page does not print a different Saturday price, so both day columns use $300–$3,100. The $4 and $15 extras are not added into the range. Food is not included.

What's included

  • Garden ceremony: 2 hours between 10 a.m. and 3 p.m., up to 50 guests, $300
  • Bauer-Neill-Mitchell House: 3 hours with tables, chairs, TV, kitchen, restroom, and patio, maximum 45 guests, $300
  • Citrus House: 3 hours with tables, chairs, TV, and a microwave, up to 50 guests, $300
  • Tropical daytime rental, 10 a.m.–4 p.m., $2,100 for up to 125 guests
  • Paradise evening rental, 5 p.m.–midnight, $3,100 for up to 125 guests

Not included

  • Guests over 125 on the Tropical or Paradise rental: $15 per person, up to 250
  • A $4-per-person catering fee on the Tropical and Paradise rentals
  • Lighting for the Paradise rental after dark. The page says the renter must bring it
  • Extra hours on the small spaces ($55, or $100 after 5 p.m. on the houses and garden)

Ask on the tour

  1. Does the $300 garden ceremony allow a reception, or ceremony only?
  2. Who may serve alcohol, and is there a permit?
  3. Are Saturday and weekday the same price? The page does not split them.
  4. Which spaces are wheelchair accessible?
