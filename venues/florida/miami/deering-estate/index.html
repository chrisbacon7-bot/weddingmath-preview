Deering Estate

HistoricWaterfrontGarden

16701 SW 72nd Avenue, Palmetto Bay, FL 33157

Published venue rental

$10,433

At 100 guests, Saturday. September 2025 facility rules PDF: Historic Grounds base rental is $9,750, plus $18 a guest over 150, plus 7% Florida sales tax. This number is that rental and tax. It does not include the published staff fee ($1,287.68 up to 150 guests) or the $5-per-person catering permit. Monday–Thursday year-round, and any day in June–August, is 25% off the base rental. That discount is not applied here, because Saturday outside summer is the full base.

What's included

  • Historic Grounds from 5:00 p.m. to 11:30 p.m.: Grand Lawn, Courtyard, Royal Palm Grove, Fig Garden, and Mango Grove
  • The base rental covers up to 150 guests
  • A ceremony-only outdoor rental is listed separately at $3,500

Not included

  • Historic houses. The 2025 rules say they stay closed for this rental
  • Staffing. Up to 150 guests the published staff fee is $1,287.68, and it is not in the card number
  • A $5-per-person catering permit, and food itself

Ask on the tour

  1. Are we in the 25% discount window (June–August, or Monday–Thursday)?
  2. What is the staff fee at our exact guest count?
  3. Is there a rain plan if the houses stay closed?
  4. What is the largest guest count they will actually book?
