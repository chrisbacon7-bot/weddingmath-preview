Vizcaya Museum and Gardens

HistoricWaterfrontGarden

3251 South Miami Ave., Miami, FL 33129

Published venue rental

$24,610

At 100 guests, Saturday. Vizcaya's own wedding page: evening base rental is $23,000 for 100 guests, 6:30–11 p.m., any day. Each guest over 100 is $25. A 7% sales tax applies to the fees, and this number includes that tax. It does not include a tent ($8,500 or $12,000), extra hours ($2,400), or catering. Capacity is 300. The daytime garden ceremony is $2,000 for up to 25 guests.

What's included

  • Evening rental of the inner courtyard, terraces, and gardens, 6:30 p.m. to 11 p.m., for 100 guests
  • Parking for 125 cars is included in the evening rental
  • A daytime garden ceremony is a separate, smaller fee

Not included

  • Food and decor. Caterers and planners must come from the approved list
  • A tent on the East Terrace. The wedding page requires one above 90 guests; the FAQ says above 100. Neither tent fee is in this number
  • Extra guests, extra hours, early load-in, and the 7% sales tax, which the page says applies

Ask on the tour

  1. Do we need the $8,500 tent or the $12,000 tent at our guest count?
  2. The main house seats 100. Are we planning the courtyard or the East Terrace?
  3. What is a typical approved-caterer minimum?
  4. Police for events over 200 guests are $150 an hour with a four-hour minimum. Do we need them?
