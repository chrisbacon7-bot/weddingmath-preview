Chicago · Wheaton

Cantigny Park

GardenHistoric

1S151 Winfield Rd, Wheaton, IL 60189

All-in estimate

$26,445–$29,445

At 100 guests, Saturday. Cantigny 2027 wedding guide, linked from cantigny.org and read October 3, 2026. Saturday venue fees are $3,000 at Le Jardin and $6,000 at Tribune Hall and the Woodside Pavilion. Friday and Sunday venue fees are lower ($2,000–$4,000) and are not this range. The Saturday food-and-beverage minimum is $18,000 at all three rooms and is not included. Tax 7.25% and a 23% service charge apply to food and beverage, not as a tax on this venue-fee range. Le Jardin holds 200. Tribune Hall and Woodside hold 250. Woodside is May 1–October 31 and family-style only.

What's included

  • A Saturday venue fee for one of three reception rooms: Le Jardin, Tribune Hall, or Woodside Pavilion

Not included

  • The Saturday food-and-beverage minimum of $18,000 at each of those three rooms
  • Illinois tax of 7.25% and a 23% service charge, which the guide says are added to food and beverage and are not in the venue fee
  • Ceremony-site fees: Prairie View $3,000, Rose Garden $3,500, Rock Garden $3,500

Ask on the tour

  1. Which room, and is the venue fee $3,000 or $6,000?
  2. Does our guest count fit under the $18,000 Saturday food minimum?
  3. Is the 23% service charge on food only?
