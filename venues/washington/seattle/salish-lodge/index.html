Salish Lodge & Spa

WaterfrontResort

6501 Railroad Ave SE, Snoqualmie, WA 98065

Published Saturday range

$4,000–$6,000

At 100 guests, Saturday. Salish Lodge 2026–2027 pricing chart, linked from the lodge's wedding page. Cliffside Ballroom Saturday facility rental is $6,000 from May 1 through October 31 and $4,000 from November 1 through April 30. The matching Saturday food-and-beverage minimums are $10,000 and $7,000. A 24% service charge is extra and is not treated as tax. Outside food and drink are not allowed. Weddings are 25 to 100 guests.

What's included

  • Cliffside Ballroom facility rental, the range below, from the 2026–2027 pricing chart linked on the wedding page
  • Five hours of event time, in-house tables, chairs, linens, and a guest room for the couple the night before and the night of
  • Centennial Green ceremony-only is listed at $1,500 on the same chart

Not included

  • The food-and-beverage minimum. Peak Saturday at Cliffside is $10,000, and November–April Saturday is $7,000
  • A 24% service charge. It is not entered as tax
  • Washington sales tax of 8.7% and a 2% lands-protection tax, which the guidelines apply to food, beverage, and audiovisual gear

Ask on the tour

  1. Is our Saturday in May–October, when the Cliffside rental is $6,000, or November–April, when it is $4,000?
  2. The contract value is rental plus the food minimum. What does a plated dinner add past $10,000?
  3. Weddings of 25 or more require a day-of coordinator. Who is ours?
