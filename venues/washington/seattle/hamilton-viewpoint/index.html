What's included

  • A ceremony permit. The park stays open to the public

Not included

  • Alcohol. Seattle park code prohibits it
  • Chairs and tables where the brochure says standing only
  • The $75 special-equipment fee for a large canopy, stage, or portable restroom
  • Picnic-shelter fees, which the brochure says may be extra

Ask on the tour

  1. Is our site a two-hour minimum or a three-hour specialty slot?
  2. Are chairs allowed here?
  3. Do we need the $75 equipment fee or extra insurance?
