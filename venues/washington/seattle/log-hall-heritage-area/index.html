Log Hall Heritage Area

Historic

202 NE Park St, Poulsbo, WA 98370

Published venue rental

$1,703

At 100 guests, Saturday. Washington State Parks Log Hall Heritage Area wedding day-use page, checked October 3, 2026. The page lists the site as good for weddings. Friday–Sunday is $1,702.65 plus tax per day. Thursdays and Mondays are $966.82 plus tax per day. Tuesday and Wednesday are not printed. The tax percent is not printed and is not added. The stored Saturday fee is the Friday–Sunday rate. The stored off-day fee is the Thursday and Monday rate, so a Friday or Sunday quote should use the higher rate, not the off-day figure. The rental includes the Log Hall, caterer’s kitchen, pavilion, and hospitality house, and accommodates up to 175 guests. A Discover Pass or day pass is required and is not added. Poulsbo is not the Seattle tax row, so this card stays a site fee.
