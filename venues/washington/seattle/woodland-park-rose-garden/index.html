Woodland Park Rose Garden

Garden

750 N 50th Street, Seattle, WA 98103

All-in estimate

$9,677–$12,545

At 100 guests, Saturday. Bramble and Wood, the zoo's wedding operator, publishes the Historic Rose Garden ceremony. Weekend (Friday–Sunday) is $3,000 for 11 a.m.–2 p.m. and $3,600 for 4–7 p.m. Weekday is $2,000 and $3,000 for those same slots. Capacity is 200 seated. Available April–September. The terrace social hour is a separate $1,000 add-on with a $1,500 food-and-beverage minimum and is not included in this range. The zoo's own roses page does not print these dollars.

What's included

  • Historic Rose Garden ceremony, April through September, with 100 white folding chairs
  • Two time slots: 11 a.m.–2 p.m. or 4–7 p.m.

Not included

  • The optional Rose Garden terrace social hour, $1,000, which is not part of the ceremony rental
  • That social hour's $1,500 food-and-beverage minimum on the limited Rose Garden menu
  • Extra tables, chairs, and audio, which are rented separately

Ask on the tour

  1. Are we in the morning slot or the 4 p.m. slot? Saturday is $3,000 or $3,600.
  2. Is the terrace social hour being added, with its $1,500 food minimum?
  3. What does Levy add for service charge and tax on food?
