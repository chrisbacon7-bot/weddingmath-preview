Highland Community Center

Ballroom

14224 NE Bel Red Road, Bellevue, WA 98007

Published venue rental

$500

At 100 guests, Saturday. Bellevue Highland Community Center page, checked October 3, 2026. The multi-purpose room, capacity 150, is for weddings and receptions. The room fee is $125 an hour. A four-hour minimum applies to all weekend and holiday rentals, so Saturday is 4 times $125, which is $500. Weekend packages at $160 and $200 an hour add the kitchen or the Fireside Room and are not this fee. A 30 percent nonprofit discount is not the base. The page does not print a weekday minimum, so no off-day total is stored.
