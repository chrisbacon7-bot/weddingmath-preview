At 100 guests, Saturday. Shemer Art Center page, checked October 3, 2026. Whether you are planning an intimate gathering or a 300-guest wedding ceremony and reception, the Shemer Art Center is a unique location at which to hold a private event. 2026 venue-only rates for up to 10 hours: Ficus Patio and Lobby $2,500 at 100 guests; Studio Courtyard $2,750 or $3,250; Lawn and Garden $3,500, $3,750, or $4,000; full facility for weddings $4,500, $4,750, or $5,000 at 100, 200, or 300 guests. Lawn maximum is 300. Rates are not split by Saturday versus weekday. A cleaning fee is extra and not included. Pricing not published, ask the venue.
