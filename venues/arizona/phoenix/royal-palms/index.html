What's included

  • Three hosted wedding packages in the 2026–2027 PDF: $255, $275, and $310 per person
  • Each includes dinner, cake, and a hosted deluxe bar for three, four, or five hours
  • Alegria Garden is listed for up to 180 guests. A wedding suite and a junior suite are included for the weekend

Not included

  • A flat Saturday room total. The PDF prices the packages per person
  • A ceremony line of $8,000 sits in an unlabeled column beside drink prices, so it is not used as the ceremony fee
  • Tax and any service charge, which the PDF does not state as a percent

Ask on the tour

  1. Which hour length is ours: $255, $275, or $310 a person?
  2. What is the ceremony fee? The $8,000 figure is not clearly tied to one lawn.
  3. Is Saturday the same per-person price as Friday?
