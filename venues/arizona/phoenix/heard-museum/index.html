What's included

  • One evening space for up to 300 guests: the contracted room, gallery access, and security and facilities staff
  • Evening hours are 6–11 p.m. Friday and Saturday, and 6–10 p.m. Sunday–Thursday

Not included

  • Tables, chairs, linen, and equipment
  • A second space, listed at $1,500, and each extra 100 guests, listed at $500
  • Exclusive use of the whole museum, which the brochure says to ask about

Ask on the tour

  1. Which single space is $7,000 on our date, the auditorium or a courtyard?
  2. Exclusive evening use is not on the fee list. What does that cost?
  3. Who may cater, and is there a minimum?
  4. Amplified music is restricted outdoors. Does that change the plan?
