Published event minimum

$50,000

At 100 guests, Saturday. El Chorro 2026 wedding-venues PDF. Casa Paloma or the Event Lawn has a Saturday food-and-beverage minimum of $50,000. Friday is $40,000. Sunday–Thursday is $30,000. The evening per-person minimum is $175, and the higher of the two applies. Tax and the service charge are extra. The PDF states the service charge as 28% in one place and 27% in another, so it is not added. The Event Lawn ceremony fee is $7,000 on Saturday and is not inside the $50,000.

What's included

  • Casa Paloma or Event Lawn Saturday food-and-beverage minimum, the floor below
  • Reception includes house tables, chiavari chairs, linen, china, and glassware
  • Event Lawn ceremony fee is a separate $7,000 on Saturday and $6,000 Sunday–Friday, for up to 250

Not included

  • The 8.8% sales tax and the service charge. The PDF prints that charge as both 27% and 28%, so neither is entered as tax
  • A per-person minimum of $175, which replaces the floor when it is higher
  • Seasonal pricing for January, February, June, July, and August, which the PDF says to ask about
  • Tenting if the weather turns. The client pays for the tent

Ask on the tour

  1. At our guest count, is the bill the $50,000 Saturday floor or $175 a person, whichever is greater?
  2. The PDF says 28% in the price header and 27% in the policy section. Which service charge is on the contract?
  3. Do we also need the $7,000 Saturday ceremony fee?
