Merchant's House Museum

HistoricGardenMuseum

29 East Fourth Street, New York, NY 10003

Published Saturday range

$1,800–$5,500

At 100 guests, Saturday. Merchant's House wedding page. A 90-minute ceremony for up to 10 people is $1,800. A two-hour ceremony for up to 30 is $4,000. A three-hour ceremony with one caterer's table for up to 30 is $5,500. The page does not split Saturday from other days. A one-hour, five-person fee of $750 is also listed and is not the top of this range. The separate still-photography rate of $300 to $750 an hour is not a wedding price and is not used. Food is not included. Tax is not listed. The house was under restoration scaffolding through October 2026.

What's included

  • Gold folding chairs set by museum staff
  • The garden or the Greek Revival double parlor
  • A one-year museum membership, which the guidelines value at $500, inside the rental fee

Not included

  • Food and drink on ceremony-only bookings
  • An extra $500 if a caterer serves the three-hour toast, and another $500 if the museum closes at 4 p.m.
  • A $500 damage deposit, overtime at $250 per half hour, and a 30% non-refundable deposit

Ask on the tour

  1. Which booking is ours: $1,800 for 90 minutes and 10 people, $4,000 for two hours and 30, or $5,500 for three hours and a toast?
  2. Scaffolding is scheduled through October 2026. Is the garden usable on our date?
  3. The garden cannot be tented. Is the parlor the rain plan we want?
  4. The $750 one-hour fee for five people is a different booking. Is that an elopement or a photo hour?
