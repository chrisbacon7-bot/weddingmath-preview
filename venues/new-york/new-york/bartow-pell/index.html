What's included

  • Conservatory, Lachaise Lawn, or Pebble Court, with tables, chairs, and white linens
  • A tent on the Lachaise Lawn and on the Pebble Court
  • Prep time before the event and an hour of cleanup after

Not included

  • China, glassware, catering, wait staff, music, and flowers
  • A refundable security deposit of $250 or $500
  • Special-event insurance, and $500 an hour if vendors need extra setup time

Ask on the tour

  1. Are we booking the $3,000 conservatory, the $7,500 lawn, or the $10,000 Pebble Court?

  3. Hard liquor needs a licensed caterer. Is a one-day beer and wine permit enough for our bar?
