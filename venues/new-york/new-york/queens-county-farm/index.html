Queens County Farm Museum

GardenHistoric

73-50 Little Neck Parkway, Floral Park, NY 11004

Published Saturday range

$1,450–$5,200

At 100 guests, Saturday. Queens County Farm Museum's 2025 site-rental PDF, still the fee sheet on the museum's site. The Pavilion is $1,450, the North Lawn and the Pumpkin Patch are $2,200 each, and the Orchard and the Barn Reception Hall are $5,200 each, all for four party hours. The sheet does not publish a Saturday versus weekday split. It does not include tents on the outdoor sites or catering. The barn and north lawn are marked under construction beginning May 2025. Tax is not listed. The farm is in Queens, at the Floral Park mailing address on the PDF.

What's included

  • Four party hours and two to three site staff
  • Barn rentals include tables, 120 chairs, and the food-prep kitchen
  • Weekend setup from 10 a.m., and weekday setup from 1:30 p.m.

Not included

  • Tents, tables, and chairs on the lawns, which must come from Ace Party & Tent
  • Catering, parking fees, and a getting-ready room at $300
  • Overtime at $300 or $400 an hour

Ask on the tour

  1. The posted sheet is the 2025 rate PDF. Is a 2026 sheet in use for our date?
  2. The barn and north lawn were marked under construction beginning May 2025. Are they open?
  3. September and October are not available. Does our date fall in the April–August season?
  4. Which space is the fee: Pavilion $1,450, lawn or pumpkin patch $2,200, or orchard or barn $5,200?
