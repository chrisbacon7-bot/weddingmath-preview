At 100 guests, Saturday. Brooklyn Botanic Garden ceremony page, open October 3, 2026. Weekend permit is $628 for up to 50 people, nonrefundable, 9–10 a.m. only. Up to 10 more guests are $22 each, 60 maximum. That per-person add-on is not applied automatically, because it stops at 10 people. What's included

  • Weekend 9 a.m. ceremony permit for up to 50 people
  • Choice of Fragrance Garden, Japanese Hill-and-Pond Garden, Osborne Garden, Plant Family Collection, Rose Garden, or Water Garden
  • Portrait photography during the booking

Not included

  • A seated reception. Full catering is a separate inquiry and is not priced on this page
  • Chairs. Ceremonies are stand-up
  • Food
  • More than 10 guests past 50. Each of those is $22, and 60 is the maximum
