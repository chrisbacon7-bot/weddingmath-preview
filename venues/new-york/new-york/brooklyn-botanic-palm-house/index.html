All-in breakdown

All-in $92,250 for 100 guests on Saturday, peak season. Estimated. Refundable deposits are not in the total. Prices change. Confirm with the venue before booking.

Needs a human look: Room rental and the food minimum are Palm House only. The Atrium on the same kit is a smaller room and is not mixed into this total. The 26% administrative fee is applied to the food line because the kit says the food minimum is before that fee. It does not say the percent also applies to the room rental, so the rental is not marked up. Off-day food uses the May–October Sunday minimum. Other Sunday minimums are lower and stay in the note.

Sales tax 8.875% $6,150 Estimated

Estimated from the city's 8.875% rate for restaurant and caterer food and beverages. The same percent is also applied to the service-charge line, because TB-ST-806 says a service charge that is not identified as a gratuity is taxable. A charge that is separately stated, called a gratuity, and paid entirely to employees is not given this treatment.

“NYC Department of Finance: food and beverages sold by restaurants and caterers are taxed at a total sales and use tax of 8.875 percent (city 4.5%, state 4%, MCTD 0.375%). Tax Bulletin TB-ST-806: separately stated service charges or other charges not specifically listed as gratuities on a bill or invoice are subject to sales tax.”

Source · 2026-10-03

What's included

  • Palm House room rental for a five-hour event, with load-in five hours before guest arrival
  • Tables, white linens, ceremony chairs, chiavari reception chairs, china, flatware, and glassware listed in the kit
  • The wedding-day fee: a VIP server, ceremony chair setup, coat check, and restroom attendants

Not included

  • Food and the open bar, which are the minimum or the $228 starting package, whichever is higher
  • The 26% administrative fee and sales tax
  • The $800 Patron membership the kit requires
  • The Atrium, which is a smaller room with its own rental

Ask on the tour

  1. Are we in May, June, September, or October, or a lower Saturday season?
  2. Will the guest count clear the $55,000 food minimum, or stay on the $228 starting package?
  3. The Atrium seats 125 and costs less. Is Palm House the room we want?
