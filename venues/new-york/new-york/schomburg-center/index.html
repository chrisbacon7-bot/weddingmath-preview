Estimated from the city's 8.875% rate for restaurant and caterer food and beverages. The same percent is also applied to the service-charge line, because TB-ST-806 says a service charge that is not identified as a gratuity is taxable. “NYC Department of Finance: food and beverages sold by restaurants and caterers are taxed at a total sales and use tax of 8.875 percent (city 4.5%, state 4%, MCTD 0.375%). Tax Bulletin TB-ST-806: separately stated service charges or other charges not specifically listed as gratuities on a bill or invoice are subject to sales tax.”

What's included

  • The room rental only, for the Langston Hughes Auditorium at the individual and nonprofit Friday–Saturday rate
  • The auditorium listing includes 321 seats plus 4 wheelchair spaces

Not included

  • Maintenance, security, audio-visual, and administrative costs, which the page says are extra
  • Catering. The center does not provide it and does not hold a liquor license
  • The corporate rate, which is higher and is not used here

Ask on the tour

  1. The Friday–Saturday column is one price. Is a Friday actually the same $4,250 as Saturday?
  2. Sunday for this room is $5,250, higher than Saturday. Are we looking at a Sunday?
  3. Which technicians are required, and what do the hourly engineering and security rates add?
  4. Is a wedding allowed in the auditorium, or only in the lobby or courtyard?
