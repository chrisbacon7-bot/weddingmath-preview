Brooklyn Bridge Park

WaterfrontGarden

Brooklyn Bridge Park, Brooklyn, NY

Published venue rental

$426

At 100 guests, Saturday. Brooklyn Bridge Park's wedding permit page: a $400 site fee, a $25 application fee, and a $1 credit-card fee. The full $426 is charged when the application is submitted and is non-refundable. The fee is not split by day of the week. It is a ceremony permit only, for up to 100 guests and 1.5 hours. It does not include a reception, catering, or alcohol.

What's included

  • A 1.5-hour ceremony permit at Pier 1 Granite Prospect, Empire Fulton Ferry Boardwalk, or Main Street Pebble Beach
  • The permit is required even for fewer than 20 guests
  • The park states that the entire park is ADA accessible

Not included

  • A reception. Receptions are not allowed
  • Chairs beyond 10 folding chairs for guests who need them, tents, staging, or amplified sound
  • Exclusive use. The promenade at Pier 1 must stay open to the public

Ask on the tour

  1. Which of the three ceremony spots is open on our date?
  2. Applications for the next calendar year open December 1. Are we inside that window?
  3. Holidays and holiday weekends are not permitted. Does our weekend count?
  4. Where do guests go if it rains? The permit has no indoor backup.
