Sales tax 8.875%$1,544–$2,074Estimated

Estimated from the city's 8.875% rate for restaurant and caterer food and beverages. The same percent is also applied to the service-charge line, because TB-ST-806 says a service charge that is not identified as a gratuity is taxable. A charge that is separately stated, called a gratuity, and paid entirely to employees is not given this treatment.

“NYC Department of Finance: food and beverages sold by restaurants and caterers are taxed at a total sales and use tax of 8.875 percent (city 4.5%, state 4%, MCTD 0.375%). Tax Bulletin TB-ST-806: separately stated service charges or other charges not specifically listed as gratuities on a bill or invoice are subject to sales tax.”
