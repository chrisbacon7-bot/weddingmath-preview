Not included

  • The maintenance fee, $75 Monday–Thursday and $150 Friday–Sunday
  • Extra hours, $70 on Monday–Thursday and $105 on Friday–Sunday
  • Security monitors, which the page says are required and priced by the facility

Ask on the tour

  1. Does the $1,600 Friday–Sunday total include the $300 refundable deposit the footnote describes?
  2. Will the event use the contracted bar?
  3. Is the guest count the indoor 70 or the combined 120?
