Published Saturday range

$5,800–$10,300

At 100 guests, Saturday. City of Beverly Hills special-event pricing for Greystone, estimates valid for events booked through June 30, 2027. A wedding with ceremony and reception runs from $5,800 for Beverly Hills residents, 1–75 guests, 4 hours, to $10,300 for non-residents, 76–150 guests, 6 hours. The table is not split by Saturday. It includes ranger, maintenance, and kitchen-cleaning fees paid to Greystone. It does not include catering or refundable deposits. The hourly photo permit is not used as a wedding price.

What's included

  • The Formal Garden for the ceremony and the courtyards for the reception
  • Four hours of vendor setup, two hours of breakdown, one prep room, and the parking lot
  • Park rangers, the maintenance fee, and kitchen cleaning, which the city says are inside the estimate

Not included

  • Catering, tables, chairs, and a required wedding coordinator
  • Refundable deposits
  • A separate ceremony-only rental, listed from $2,200 to $3,400

Ask on the tour

  1. Are we Beverly Hills residents or non-residents, and is the event 4, 5, or 6 hours?
  2. The table is an estimate through June 30, 2027. What is the exact permit total?
  3. Receptions cap at 150. Ceremony-only goes to 250. Which booking is this?
  4. Photography permits are $330 or $413 an hour and are not a wedding. Are we booking the event instead?
