What's included

  • Mini Masterpiece ceremony for up to 20 guests and up to 2 hours
  • Two pedestals, two floral arrangements, a seasonal bouquet, a seasonal boutonniere, and setup coordinated to the museum timeline
  • Choice of Classic Luxe, Modern Elegance, or Timeless Heritage chair styles

Not included

  • A meal. A celebratory toast is $650 extra
  • A ceremonial arch or chuppah, $1,000 or $1,750
  • Larger reception pricing. The event-rental guide tells you to inquire and does not print a reception rental

Ask on the tour

  1. The three prices on each room are the three looks, not Saturday versus Friday. Which look is the contract using?
  2. Panama 66 stays open during Sculpture Garden ceremonies. Does that change privacy or the bar?
  3. What is the Saturday price for a full reception, as opposed to this 20-guest ceremony?
