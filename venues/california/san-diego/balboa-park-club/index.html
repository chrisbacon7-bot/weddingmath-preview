What's included

  • The city fee schedule lists the Balboa Park Club ballroom with a 3-hour minimum
  • Balboa Park's private-events page lists the club ballroom among city buildings and shows a capacity of 500

Not included

  • The schedule prints three hourly columns and does not say which one a wedding uses
  • Kitchen rental is separate: $23, $47, or $70 per hour on the same three columns
  • Custodial overtime if the rental is outside normal hours, charged for two custodians

Ask on the tour

  1. A private wedding is not labeled on the schedule. Are we the nonprofit, non-commercial, or commercial column?
  2. The ballroom is hourly with a 3-hour minimum. How many hours does a reception need, and is Saturday the same rate?
  3. Is alcohol allowed in the club, and who may cater?
