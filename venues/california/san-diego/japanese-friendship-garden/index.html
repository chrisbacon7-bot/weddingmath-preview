San Diego · San Diego

Japanese Friendship Garden

GardenArt

2215 Pan American Road E, San Diego, CA 92101

Published venue rental

$1,400

At 100 guests, Saturday. Lower Garden page. Friday–Sunday is $1,400 per hour with a six-hour minimum that includes vendor setup and breakdown. Monday–Thursday is $1,100 per hour. The numbers here are the hourly rates, not a six-hour total. The Inamori Pavilion is an optional $1,500 add-on for one side. Seated dinner inside is about 100 with a dance floor, or 150 if tables also use the Koi Deck. Cocktail capacity on one pavilion side is 250. A minimony page lists separate daytime ceremony prices of $5,000 Friday–Sunday, including a $600 refundable deposit, and those are a different product.

What's included

  • Lower Garden outdoor areas at the hourly rate
  • The Koi Deck is covered and the page says it can hold more than 100 chairs if it rains
  • A one-hour rehearsal for a full-scale wedding, scheduled during public hours, on the FAQ page

Not included

  • The Inamori Pavilion, a $1,500 flat add-on for one side. It is optional
  • Upper Garden Fujidana Plaza, a $2,000 flat add-on
  • The six-hour Friday–Sunday minimum is a booking rule. This card does not multiply the hourly rate by six

Ask on the tour

  1. Friday–Sunday is $1,400 per hour with a six-hour minimum. Is the contract $8,400 before the pavilion, or do some of those hours bill differently?
  2. Do we need the Inamori Pavilion at $1,500 for a seated dinner?
  3. Who may cater and serve alcohol, and when can vendors arrive before 3pm?
