San Diego · Encinitas

San Diego Botanic Garden

Garden

300 Quail Gardens Dr, Encinitas, CA 92024

Published Saturday range

$8,000–$8,500

At 100 guests, Saturday. San Diego Botanic Garden rental page, open October 3, 2026. Saturday evening exclusive rental is $8,500 from March through October and $8,000 from November through February. Friday and Sunday evenings are $8,000 or $7,500 by the same seasons. The page says pricing is subject to change. Daytime ceremony-and-reception of the Walled Garden and Lawn House Garden is $5,000 March–October and $4,500 November–February. Daytime ceremony only runs from $1,800 at the Waterfall Deck (30 guests) to $4,000 on the Gazebo Lawn. This card is the Saturday evening span only. Gazebo Lawn reception capacity is 200 seated. Encinitas is inside the San Diego metro.

What's included

  • Evening exclusive rental of the Walled Garden for the ceremony, the Herb Garden for cocktail hour, and the Gazebo Lawn for the reception
  • Ceremony chairs and belly-bar tables for cocktail hour

Not included

  • Dinner tables and chairs beyond the cocktail-hour belly bars
  • A $400 fee to use the Lawn House Garden for cocktail hour
  • A $600 early-start fee at 4 p.m. on Friday or Saturday
  • Daytime-only prices, which are lower and listed in the note

Ask on the tour

  1. Is our date March–October ($8,500 Saturday) or November–February ($8,000 Saturday)?
  2. Do we need dinner-table rentals on top of the belly bars?
  3. Is the Dickinson Conservatory a separate rental?
