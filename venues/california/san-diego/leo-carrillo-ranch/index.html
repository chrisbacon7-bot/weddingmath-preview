What's included

  • The pricing page says catering is included in the package and that Personal Touch Dining manages the ranch
  • Timing listed: 1-hour rehearsal, 2 hours in dressing rooms, 5 hours on the grounds, 2 hours setup, and 1 hour cleanup
  • Hacienda courtyard and patio: 200 seated or 275 reception-style. On-site parking

Not included

  • The package price. The page says to contact them for the brochure
  • Published add-ons that are not the package: $125 fuel surcharge, $95 sustainability fee, $200 insurance, $425 security, and a $200 outside-vendor fee
  • A refundable $500 damage deposit. The page also says items are subject to a 23 percent production fee and California sales tax, without printing the package those percents apply to

Ask on the tour

  1. What is the Saturday all-inclusive package, and what is a Thursday?
  2. The 23 percent production fee and sales tax need a base price. What are they applied to?
  3. Liquor is in-house and beer and wine can be client-provided but ranch-served. What is the bar price?
