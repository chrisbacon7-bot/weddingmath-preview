Balboa Park Administration Courtyard

Garden

2125 Park Blvd, San Diego, CA 92101

Published venue rental

$191

At 100 guests, Saturday. Same city grounds-use fee as the other Balboa ceremony lawns: $191 per day for other users with 50–300 people. The wedding-sites PDF says this courtyard is permitted only on Saturdays and Sundays, capacity 200, ceremonies only, no food or beverages. Sunday uses the same published daily fee, so the off-day number matches Saturday. Nonprofit adult and government at this size is $133. The sites PDF's hourly wording does not include its own dollar table.
