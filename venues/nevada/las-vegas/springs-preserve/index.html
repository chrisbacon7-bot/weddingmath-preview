Springs Preserve

Garden

333 S Valley View Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89107

Published Saturday range

$5,175–$5,665

At 100 guests, Saturday. Springs Preserve wedding page, open October 3, 2026. Thursday–Monday packages, same price every one of those days: Garden Terrace $5,175 for up to 100, Courtyard $5,575 for up to 200, Agave Room $5,665 for up to 120. Ceremony-only on Thursday and Sunday is $1,565 at the Gazebo (chairs not included) or $1,775 at the Garden Arbor, and is not this range. Friday and Saturday ceremony-only can be reserved only inside 180 days; those rates are not printed separately. Alcohol must come from a licensed caterer. The street address is the Preserve's public address; the wedding page itself did not reprint it.

What's included

  • Ready room, ceremony, and reception time
  • Preset tables and chairs, with the limits in the note
  • Guest parking and directional signs
  • The Preserve's service fees, which the page says are included in the package

Not included

  • A licensed wedding coordinator, which is required and not priced
  • Catering. Any caterer is a separate contract
  • Security deposit, which starts at $250
  • Holiday surcharges, which are mentioned without a dollar amount
  • AV equipment

Ask on the tour

  1. Which package: Garden Terrace $5,175, Courtyard $5,575, or Agave $5,665?
  2. Who is the coordinator, and what is their fee?
  3. Is our date a holiday with an extra fee?
