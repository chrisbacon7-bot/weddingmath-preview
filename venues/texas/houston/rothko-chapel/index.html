Rothko Chapel

Art

3900 Yupon Street, Houston, TX 77006

Published venue rental

$3,000

At 100 guests, Saturday. Rothko Chapel private-services page. A wedding ceremony is $3,000 for up to 75 guests. From 76 to 100 guests is $100 more for the whole band, and from 101 to 125 is $200 more for the whole band. Those are not per-guest rates, so they are not entered as an extra-guest fee. The dollar amount does not change by day. No reception is allowed on site. A $750 deposit holds the date.

What's included

  • A wedding ceremony of up to 60 minutes between 10 a.m. and 6 p.m. The base fee is for up to 75 guests
  • A chapel representative on site
  • Rehearsal the day before from 6 to 7 p.m. is encouraged

Not included

  • A reception. Ceremonies and receptions are not permitted on the plaza or grounds
  • Food and drink inside the chapel
  • Photography and video inside the building
  • The $750 nonrefundable deposit, which is part of reserving the date and is not an extra rental
  • Sound technician at $200, and the office annex at $125 an hour

Ask on the tour

  1. The base fee is $3,000 up to 75 guests. We are above that. Is the add-on $100 or $200 for our count?
  2. The fee does not change by day. Is a Saturday in the next six months still open? Bookings cannot be made further out.
  3. Where can the reception be, since it cannot be on the grounds?
