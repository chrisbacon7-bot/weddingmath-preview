All-in estimate

$11,180–$13,556

At 100 guests, Saturday. From the Garden's 2026 private rentals guide. Woodland Glade, a 4-hour rental seating up to 200, is Saturday $4,200, Friday or Sunday $3,250, and Monday–Thursday $1,800. The climate-controlled Global Collection Garden Tent is Saturday $5,700 and Monday–Friday or Sunday $4,500, with admission tickets for up to 200. The FAQ says weekday Woodland Glade ceremonies start at $1,800 and the large tent starts at $4,500. Food is not in the rental. The price table is dense, so treat $4,200 as the Saturday lawn rental and confirm the hour block when you inquire.

What's included

  • Woodland Glade 4-hour rental, the ceremony lawn used for the number below. The guide says it seats up to 200
  • The Garden says a ceremony there can include a private entrance, parking, and a rehearsal hour on some packages
  • The FAQ says the garden is ADA compliant and has on-site parking

Not included

  • Food and furniture, which must come from approved vendors
  • A security guard when alcohol is served: $50 an hour, four-hour minimum, one guard per 100 guests
  • The Global Collection Garden Tent, priced separately
