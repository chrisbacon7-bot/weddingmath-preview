What's included

  • The quoted room is the Grand Hall for four hours
  • The PDF says the fee includes on-site security, a building supervisor, janitorial service, and maintenance
  • Evening weddings also include an onsite wedding manager and house staff, per the museum FAQ

Not included

  • Food, drinks, tables, chairs, and linens. The FAQ says those come from one of four caterers
  • Other halls. Wiess Energy Hall and the exhibit floors are priced separately
  • December, when the Grand Hall rate on the same PDF is $5,500

Ask on the tour

  1. Is the Grand Hall the right room, or do we need a hall with a ceremony capacity note?
  2. Our date is in December. Is the $5,500 rate the one that applies?
  3. Members get 10% off the facility rental. Do we qualify?
  4. What is the caterer minimum for 100 guests?
