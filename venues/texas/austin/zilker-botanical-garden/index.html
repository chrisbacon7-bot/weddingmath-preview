What's included

  • Garden ceremony and reception sites, including the Memorial Arch, Promenade, Alcove, South Lawn, and Oak Grove
  • The 2026 guidebook says private events are limited to 100 people
  • Outdoor rentals generally end at sunset, except the Oak Grove, which has lights

Not included

  • Staffing at $60 an hour for two staff, a maintenance fee, and refundable deposits
  • An alcohol deposit of $330 when alcohol is served
  • A second space if you want an indoor backup. The garden does not automatically move an event inside
  • Tables and chairs are not listed as included for the outdoor ceremony sites

Ask on the tour

  1. Are we residents? The Promenade reception minimum is printed as $1,350 for residents, and the non-resident wedding figure is not clearly lined up in the guidebook
  2. Is the Oak Grove 4-hour wedding rate $450, the same figure printed beside the party and memorial columns?
  3. Which staffing hours are added on top of the grounds fee for our timeline?
