What's included

  • The Contemporary Austin's lakeside site: Driscoll Villa, sculpture grounds, and Lake Austin
  • Ceremonies, receptions, and elopements. Tours by appointment with the rentals office
  • Inquiries go to rent@thecontemporaryaustin.org

Not included

  • A rental price. The site-rentals page does not list a fee or a guest cap
  • A photography session, which is booked separately and is not a wedding rental

Ask on the tour

  1. What is the Saturday wedding fee, and what is a weekday?
