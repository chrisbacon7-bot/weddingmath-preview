What's included

  • Terrace, lawns, ceremony arbor, garden, catering kitchen, and the Crenshaw Room
  • Security, a venue manager, a one-hour rehearsal, and a one-year dual membership
  • Seated dinner up to 250. The arbor holds about 200 for vows. The lawns can be tented

Not included

  • The rental price. The page says to submit an inquiry for rates
  • A tent. The garden recommends one and does not provide it
  • Tables and chairs, which the policies page treats as an added rental

Ask on the tour

  1. What is the Saturday evening fee, and what is a Monday daytime rate?
  2. What is the current fee for the garden's tables and chairs?
  3. Which tent company is allowed, and when can a tent be installed?
