Dallas Arboretum

Garden

Dallas, TX

Published venue rental

$13,500

At 100 guests, Saturday. From the Dallas Arboretum 2024–2026 wedding guide. The evening reception block for up to 300 seated guests (350 with a DJ) is peak $13,500 and off-peak months $11,500, plus a $1,000 deposit. The guide's column order places 'Rosine Hall & Jonsson Color Garden or Crape Myrtle Allee' immediately after that price, and lists Jonsson Color Garden on its own at peak $18,500 for up to 500. Sunday–Friday is a published 30% venue-rental discount, so the off-day figure here is 30% off $13,500. It does not also cut the seasonal off-peak rate. Peak months are March–May and September–December.

What's included

  • Rosine Hall evening ceremony and reception in peak season, used for the number below
  • The guide says each ceremony garden is included with the reception rental
  • Tables, chairs, a wedding sales manager, an event facilitator, security, and a self-guided rehearsal are listed as included with a rental

Not included

  • Valet, outdoor lighting, stage, dance floor, and draping, listed as add-ons
  • Catering. Food is not in the rental
  • A $1,000 refundable security deposit

Ask on the tour

  1. Is Rosine Hall the $13,500 peak room, or does that rate belong to the room beside it on the PDF?
  2. Does the 30% Sunday–Friday discount apply on top of the off-peak month rate?
  3. Which caterers are approved, and what is a typical per-person price?
  4. What is the rain plan for an outdoor ceremony?
