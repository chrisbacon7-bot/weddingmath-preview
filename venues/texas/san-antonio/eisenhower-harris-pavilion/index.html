Harris Pavilion at Eisenhower Park

Garden

19399 NW Military Hwy, San Antonio, TX 78257

All-in estimate

$6,074–$7,946

At 100 guests, Saturday. City page, one-day Harris Pavilion and picnic facility, commercial rate for 400 or fewer people: $575. Nonprofit at that size is $450. Commercial rates rise to $1,100 for 401–1,000 and $1,700 for 1,001 or more. The pavilion alone, under 400 and without the kitchen, is $275. The page does not charge a different Saturday rate. Staff is an extra $25 per hour and the damage deposit is extra. Those are not added into the $575. Alcohol is prohibited.

What's included

  • A one-day rental of the Harris Pavilion and picnic facility. The commercial rate used below is the 400-or-fewer tier
  • The park page notes handicapped parking. Alcohol is prohibited in the park

Not included

  • On-site staff at $25 per hour
  • The clean-up and damage deposit: $500 at this attendance tier
  • The kitchen, if you take the smaller pavilion-only option

Ask on the tour

  1. Are we a commercial rental at $575 or a nonprofit rental at $450 for 400 or fewer?
  2. Is the $275 pavilion-only rate, without the kitchen, enough for a ceremony?
  3. The park prohibits alcohol. Is that a firm rule for a reserved pavilion too?
