Japanese Tea Garden

GardenHistoric

3853 N St. Mary's St, San Antonio, TX 78212

Published venue rental

$2,500

At 100 guests, Saturday. 2026 garden rental fees on the San Antonio Parks Foundation page. The four-hour evening rental, 6pm–10pm, is $2,500 every day. A two-hour evening rental, 6pm–8pm, is $1,000. Daytime one-hour rentals are $300 at the waterfall, $400 at the Historic Mexican Village, and $400 at the pavilion. The table says chairs are not included, an extra pavilion hour is $235, and security is an extra charge that is not given a dollar amount. The evening pavilion and upper garden are listed for up to 300 guests. This is the garden rental, not a Jingu House meal.
