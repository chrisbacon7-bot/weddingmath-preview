San Antonio · San Antonio

McNay Art Museum

MuseumArtHistoricGarden

6000 N New Braunfels Ave, San Antonio, TX 78209

Published venue rental

$5,500

At 100 guests, Saturday. Mansion Reception Package or Contemporary Reception Package from the McNay wedding page. Saturday is $5,500, Friday or Sunday is $4,500, and Wednesday is $3,500. The Ultimate package that combines both sides is $8,000 on Saturday, $6,500 Friday or Sunday, and $5,000 Wednesday. A ceremony is an extra $400, and Leeper Auditorium adds a $250 cleaning fee. Neither is included in the $5,500. Couples may bring alcohol if an approved caterer or approved bar company serves it. Rates effective July 2025. Tax is not listed on the package table.

What's included

  • Mansion Reception Package or Contemporary Reception Package: the listed rooms, parking, and museum security
  • Mansion package includes Semmes Lobby, Blackburn Patio, Frost Octagon, and Leeper Auditorium, 250 seated with a dance floor
  • One hour in a designated gallery, subject to the exhibition schedule

Not included

  • A ceremony, which the wedding page prices at an extra $400
  • The $250 cleaning fee on packages that include Leeper Auditorium
  • Approved catering, the catering venue fee (the wedding page does not print the percent), tables, chairs, a planner, insurance, and off-duty police

Ask on the tour

  1. Are we booking the Mansion or Contemporary package at $5,500 on Saturday, or the Ultimate package at $8,000?
  2. What is the catering venue fee percent, and which approved caterer is available on our date?
  3. The ceremony-only grounds option is $400 for up to 50 people with no chairs. Do we need the indoor reception rain plan instead?
