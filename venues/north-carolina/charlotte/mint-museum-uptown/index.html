Mint Museum Uptown

MuseumArt

500 South Tryon Street, Charlotte, NC 28202

Published venue rental

$6,250

At 100 guests, Saturday. 2026 Mint Museum Uptown special-events brochure. Silverman Grand Room and Trustees Terrace: Saturday $6,250, Friday $5,625 after the Friday discount printed in the brochure, Sunday $4,750. The same brochure's Saturday list is $5,650 for Van Allen Terrace, Carroll Gallery, and Atrium, and $3,500 for the Queen's Room, which is not a stand-alone Friday or Saturday rental. Seated with a dance floor in the Grand Room is 250. Saturday alcohol minimum is $3,500 and is not added here. Sales tax of 8.25 percent is printed on drink prices, not on the room rental, so it is not stored as a tax rate. A 15 percent holiday-weekend upcharge and a $500 per hour fee after 11pm are separate.

What's included

  • Saturday rental of the Mattye and Marc Silverman Grand Room and Trustees Terrace, the room used for the number below
  • Wedding clients get tables, a bridal suite and groom's room when the ceremony is at the museum, a one-hour rehearsal if the museum can offer it, and the catering kitchen
  • White folding chairs are complimentary on Friday and Sunday, and for a Saturday ceremony if gold chiavari chairs are rented for the reception

Not included

  • The Saturday beverage minimum of $3,500. The museum is the exclusive alcohol supplier
  • Security and housekeeping outside museum hours at $150 per hour
  • Gold chiavari chairs, a month-of coordinator, and parking coupons. Validated garage parking is $10

Ask on the tour

  1. Are we in the Silverman Grand Room at $6,250 on Saturday, or the Atrium plus Van Allen Terrace at $5,650?
  2. The Queen's Room is $3,500 on Saturday and is not a stand-alone Friday or Saturday rental. Can it be added?
  3. What is the exclusive caterer's food minimum, separate from the $3,500 Saturday bar minimum?
