What's included

  • The 2026 events PDF describes a signature wedding collection using the Grand Lobby and Plaza Terrace for the reception
  • Grand Lobby and Plaza Terrace capacity on the page is 375 reception or 250 seated
  • Something Classic is the exclusive caterer. The museum holds the alcohol permit

Not included

  • Gallery viewing, which is an additional fee that the PDF does not price
  • Bartenders, glassware, non-alcoholic drinks, and mixers, which are arranged through Something Classic
  • A 3.5 percent credit-card fee. That is a card fee, not sales tax
