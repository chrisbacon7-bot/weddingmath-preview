Mint Museum Randolph

MuseumArtHistoricGarden

2730 Randolph Road, Charlotte, NC 28207

Published venue rental

$2,200

At 100 guests, Saturday. 2026 Randolph brochure. Wallace Atrium is $2,200 Friday–Saturday and $1,900 Sunday–Thursday on the room list. The Friday wedding rate for that atrium is $1,980 and the Sunday wedding rate is $1,710, both after the 10 percent wedding discount the brochure says is already calculated. Delhom Gallery is $2,100 Friday–Saturday. Saturday alcohol minimum is $2,500 and is not added. The 8.25 percent sales tax in the brochure is on drink prices, not the room, so it is not stored as a tax rate. Atrium cocktail capacity is 150 and seated dinner is 80.

What's included

  • Friday–Saturday rate for the Nancy A. and J. Mason Wallace Atrium, the room used for the Saturday number
  • Tables. Friday and Sunday weddings also list complimentary white folding chairs and a bottle of champagne for the couple
  • Complimentary parking at Randolph Road

Not included

  • Saturday beverage minimum of $2,500. The museum sells the alcohol
  • White padded chairs at $3 each on the standard rate, and a $400 daytime housekeeping setup fee with a 4-hour minimum
  • Front terrace, eagle terrace, and front lawn, which are not stand-alone rentals

Ask on the tour

  1. The wedding discount list prices Friday and Sunday, not Saturday. Is Saturday the standard Friday–Saturday atrium rate of $2,200?
  2. Can the front lawn at $2,500 on Friday–Saturday be added, and is a tent required for rain?
  3. Which caterers are on the preferred list, and what must an outside caterer provide?
