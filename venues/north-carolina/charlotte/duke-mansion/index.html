Charlotte · Charlotte

Duke Mansion

HistoricEstate

400 Hermitage Road, Charlotte, NC 28207

All-in estimate

$56,769–$59,074

At 100 guests, Saturday. Duke Mansion's wedding FAQ says experiences begin at a $50,000 investment. That is the published floor, not a guest total. There is no Saturday discount. White Oak food-and-beverage minimum is $25,000 before tax and service charge, and the menu is $250 per person. Service charge is 24 percent on food and beverage. State tax is 8.25 percent, including on the service charge, plus a 1 percent food-and-beverage tax. Those percents are not stored as a rental tax because they are not a tax on the $50,000 floor. Group rooms are $269 or $239 per night before tax and are extra. All 20 rooms are required for two nights.

What's included

  • The FAQ says wedding experiences begin at a $50,000 investment
  • All 20 guest rooms must be booked Friday and Saturday. The wedding night in the Dowd Suite is complimentary
  • The published menu is $250 per person and includes passed hors d'oeuvres, a two-course plated meal, and a 5-hour standard bar

Not included

  • The estate fee itself is not printed as its own dollar amount
  • White Oak food-and-beverage minimum of $25,000 before tax and service charge. The Duke package waives that minimum
  • A tent, budgeted at a minimum of about $7,500 for the White Oak package, and valet

Ask on the tour

  1. Is the $50,000 figure the estate fee, or the whole weekend before the $250-per-person menu?
  2. What is the service-charge treatment on the estate fee, separate from the 24 percent on food and beverage?
  3. How many guests can sit if the dinner is outdoors and the tent is the rain plan?
