At 100 guests, Saturday. Foundation Center at Lafreniere Park page, checked October 3, 2026. This building is used for special events, such as parties, meetings, small weddings and more. Jefferson Parish page. The Foundation Center and Patio Garden must be rented together. The printed rate is $250 per hour with a mandatory two-hour minimum, so the flat minimum is 2 × $250 = $500. Extra hours are $150 each and are not folded into that minimum. The same minimum applies every day, so Saturday and weekday use $500. JPSO security is $45 an hour with a four-hour minimum and is not added. Address on the page is 3000 Downs Boulevard, Metairie, LA 70003. No guest cap is printed.
