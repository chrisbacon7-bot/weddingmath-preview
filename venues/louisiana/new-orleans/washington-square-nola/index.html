Washington Square

HistoricGarden

700 Elysian Fields Ave, New Orleans, LA 70117

Published Saturday range

$125–$425

At 100 guests, Saturday. City of New Orleans Parks and Parkways wedding fee table, open October 3, 2026. Washington Square uses the All Other Parkways Parks wedding fees: $125, $225, $325, or $425 by guest band, with litter deposits of $100, $200, $300, or $500. The card shows that published span because the fee depends on the guest count, not the day of the week. A $50 processing deposit is extra.
