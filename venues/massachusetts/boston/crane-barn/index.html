The Barn on the Crane Estate

HistoricGardenWaterfront

290 Argilla Road, Ipswich, MA 01938

Published venue rental

$5,000

At 100 guests, Saturday. Trustees 2027 Barn brochure. Friday through Sunday is $5,000 for a 5.5-hour rental. The fee is not higher on Saturday than on Friday or Sunday. It does not include a tent or catering. Tax is not listed. The season on the venue page is May–June and September–October.

What's included

  • A 5.5-hour rental, including 30 minutes for a ceremony in the Vegetable Garden
  • Hospitality suite from 3:30 p.m., tables and chairs, a staff liaison, and a Trustees membership
  • Two hours of vendor setup and one hour of breakdown

Not included

  • A tent. The brochure says the Barn does not include tented space
  • Catering, which the brochure says is customized
  • The non-refundable payment that holds the date

Ask on the tour
