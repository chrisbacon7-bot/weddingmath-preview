The Great House on the Crane Estate

EstateHistoricGardenWaterfront

Published venue rental

$30,000

At 100 guests, Saturday. Trustees of Reservations 2027 Great House brochure. Saturday is $30,000. Friday and Sunday are $24,000. An extra hour is $2,000. The rental includes the tent, tables, and chairs. It does not include catering. Tax is not listed. The venue page says the house is fully booked for 2026, so this is the 2027 fee. Ipswich is in the Boston-Cambridge-Newton metro.

What's included

  • A 5.5-hour rental, including 30 minutes for a ceremony on the Grand Allée or in the Italian Garden
  • Tables, chairs, lighting, and the event tent, plus hospitality suites from 4:30 p.m.
  • Two hours of vendor setup, one hour of breakdown, a staff liaison, and a Trustees membership

Not included

  • Catering. The brochure describes Gourmet Hospitality and says menu pricing is customized
  • An extra hour at $2,000
  • The non-refundable initial payment required to hold the date

Ask on the tour

  2. Sunday holidays are priced as Saturdays. Is our Sunday a holiday?
  3. Is Gourmet Hospitality required, or one caterer on a list?
  4. Ipswich is in Essex County, inside the Boston metro. Confirm 250 guests with dancing.
