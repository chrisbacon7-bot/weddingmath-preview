Boston Public Library

HistoricMuseum

700 Boylston Street, Boston, MA 02116

Published venue rental

$8,000

At 100 guests, Saturday. Boston Public Library private-event pricing guide updated June 18, 2025, the guide posted on bpl.org. The Guastavino Room Friday–Sunday evening rate is $8,000 for four hours, so Saturday is that figure. Monday–Thursday evening is $6,500. A 10% discount for January–March and for non-holiday Sundays is not applied here. The nonprofit Friday–Sunday rate is $6,400. Load-in coverage is estimated at an extra $1,500. Catering is extra. The one-hour Monday ceremony at $200, rising to $300 in 2027, is not this rental. Tax is not listed. Other rooms on the same PDF run up to $26,500 for the full McKim Building on Friday–Sunday evening.

What's included

  • A four-hour Guastavino Room rental at the Friday–Sunday evening rate
  • Library staffing for security, custodial, electrical, and HVAC, which the guide says is inside the fee

Not included

  • Catering through The Catered Affair
  • Load-in and load-out coverage, estimated in the guide at an additional $1,500
  • A one-hour Monday ceremony, which is a different product at $200 through December 2026

Ask on the tour

  1. Is the Guastavino Room the space, at $8,000 Friday–Sunday evening, or a larger room such as the McKim Building at $26,500?
  2. Sunday bookings and January–March take 10% off, and that discount does not combine with the nonprofit rate. Does our date qualify?
  3. Seated capacity is 180, or 150 with a dance floor. Which layout are we using?
  4. The one-hour Monday ceremony rises to $300 for 18 guests in 2027. Is that the booking instead of a reception?
