All-in estimate

$27,866–$31,486

At 100 guests, Saturday. Massachusetts Horticultural Society 2026 private-event rate PDF for The Gardens at Elm Bank. A nine-hour Saturday rental is $15,000 in peak season, May 22–October 12, and $13,000 in the shoulder seasons. Friday peak is $10,000 and Friday shoulder is $9,000. Sunday peak is $9,000 and Sunday shoulder is $8,000. Monday–Thursday peak is $6,000. The ceremony fee, the $1,500 tenting fee, and a 5% administrative fee are extra. The 5% fee is not a sales tax and is not entered as tax. Food is not included. Wellesley is inside the Boston metro.

What's included

  • A nine-hour rental of the Hunnewell Building or Maple Grove: three hours of vendor setup, five hours of cocktail hour and reception, and one hour of breakdown
  • A garden ceremony fee, listed separately, includes white chairs for up to 230 guests and an indoor rain location

Not included

  • The ceremony fee: $1,500 in peak season and $1,250 in shoulder season
  • A 5% administrative fee on all events
  • A tenting fee of $1,500, a dance floor at $1,000, and catering

Ask on the tour

  4. Who may cater, and is there a food minimum?
