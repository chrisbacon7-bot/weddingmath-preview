What's included

  • Lakeside pavilion for up to 200, or 250 with a tent, and a river boardwalk for about 80
  • The venue says it hosts weddings and has preferred caterers and beverage service

Not included

  • Wedding package pricing. The page prints weekend and weeknight packages and then says those prices do not reflect wedding packages
  • Cooking on site unless a preferred caterer is used

Ask on the tour

  1. What is the Saturday wedding package for the lakeside pavilion, in dollars?
