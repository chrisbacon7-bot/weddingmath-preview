What's included

  • A reception in the Bell Tower or Visitor Center Overlook includes the ceremony spot
  • Two hours to set up and one hour to tear down. Indoor tables and chairs, and security
  • Bell Tower and Visitor Center are described as ADA accessible, with elevators

Not included

  • The rental dollar amount. The page mentions standard rates and a 50% deposit but does not print them
  • Outdoor furniture. Indoor rentals include tables and chairs, not linens
  • A $500 fee to use a caterer or bartender who is not on the approved list
  • Golf cart with driver at $250, and extra hours from $250

Ask on the tour

  1. What is the Saturday fee for the Bell Tower and for the Overlook?
  2. Outdoor ceremonies are listed up to 150. What is the indoor seated count if it rains?
  3. What permit is required to serve alcohol?
