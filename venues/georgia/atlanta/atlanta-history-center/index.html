Atlanta History Center

HistoricGardenMuseum

130 West Paces Ferry Road NW, Atlanta, GA 30305

Published Saturday range

$4,500–$13,500

At 100 guests, Saturday. September 2026 wedding brochure. Saturday rates on the table run from $4,500 for McElreath Hall to $13,500 for a seasonal Saturday (mid-March through June and September through November) that includes the Swan House Gardens, the Atlanta History Museum, and the Grand Overlook. Other Saturday lines include the Grand Overlook at $6,500, the museum at $7,000, and the museum plus overlook at $9,500. The columns are easy to mix up, so this card shows the published Saturday span. Food is not included. A $750 bar setup in the gardens is extra.
