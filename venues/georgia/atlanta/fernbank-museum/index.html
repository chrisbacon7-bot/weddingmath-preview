What's included

  • Great Hall rental includes Stephensen Hall, tables, chairs, and a dance floor in the evening
  • Great Hall holds 400 seated or 600 standing after 6 p.m.
  • Terrace and Dinosaur Plaza are seasonal outdoor add-ons, March–September

Not included

  • A $4,000 beverage minimum on Saturday evening, separate from the rental minimum
  • Food
  • Exhibit access: one exhibit $1,000, or $2,500 for all indoor exhibits. Giant Screen Theater is $2,500

Ask on the tour

  1. Which rooms are in the quote, and does the total stay at the $6,000 Saturday minimum?
  2. Is the $4,000 beverage minimum before tax and gratuity, and is alcohol in-house?
  3. Who is the caterer, and is there a food minimum on top of the beverage minimum?
