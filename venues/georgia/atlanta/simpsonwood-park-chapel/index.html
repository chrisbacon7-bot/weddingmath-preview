Peachtree Corners

Published Saturday range

$500–$700

At 100 guests, Saturday. Gwinnett County premier-rentals page, checked October 3, 2026. Simpsonwood Park Chapel in Peachtree Corners is described as a vintage wedding venue. Chapel plus ready room is $125 an hour for Gwinnett residents and $175 an hour for non-residents. Friday through Sunday has a four-hour minimum, so the resident total is $500 and the non-resident total is $700. Monday through Thursday has a two-hour minimum, so the resident total is $250 and the non-resident total is $350. A $200 deposit is extra. Pavilion and grounds rates are separate. A holiday rental requires a $3,000 minimum and is not this total. Peachtree Corners is not in the tax table used here, so this card stays a site fee.
