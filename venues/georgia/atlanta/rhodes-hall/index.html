What's included

  • Saturday or Sunday 4-hour event inside an 8-hour rental, with 3 hours of setup and 1 hour of cleanup
  • Tables, basic folding chairs, 1–2 staff, one security guard, and event parking

Not included

  • Extra event hours at $500, and extra setup hours at $250
  • A $500 refundable security deposit
  • A $1,000 fee if the caterer is not on the approved list
