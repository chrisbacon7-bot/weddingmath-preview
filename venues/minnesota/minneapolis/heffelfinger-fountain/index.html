Heffelfinger Fountain

Garden

Lyndale Park, W 42nd St, Minneapolis, MN 55409

Published venue rental

$525

At 100 guests, Saturday. Heffelfinger Fountain is a listed ceremony site in Lyndale Park. Flowers and water are not guaranteed in the shoulder months. Minneapolis Park & Recreation Board wedding page, open October 3, 2026. The 3-hour outdoor ceremony permit is $525 plus tax. The page does not print the tax percent, so tax is not added. A 1-hour low-impact permit is $100 plus tax for up to 30 people, standing only. A 6-hour permit is mentioned without a dollar amount. Food, receptions, alcohol, and tents are not allowed at outdoor ceremony sites. The park stays open to other visitors. Parking is extra.
