Mt. Tabor Picnic Site B

Garden

Mt. Tabor Park, Portland, OR

All-in estimate

$6,304–$8,390

At 100 guests, Saturday. Portland Parks & Recreation 2027 wedding brochure, checked October 3, 2026. The brochure is valid for wedding permits booked through 2027. Mt. Tabor Picnic Site B is listed at a facility usage fee of $224.50 for 130 people. The figure stored here is the printed facility usage fee. It is not split by day of week, so the same figure is used on Saturday and on another day. An application processing fee is extra and is not added: $133.50 if the permit is processed more than 45 days ahead, $200.25 if less than 45 days ahead, and $266.75 if 15 days or less. A separate special-use fee is printed at $224.50 for up to 499 people and increases with attendance. A vehicle access pass is $74, and a refundable key deposit is $110. The brochure's own example for the Dawson Park gazebo adds only the application fee to the facility usage fee. Those extras are not added here. Deposits printed beside a site are not added. Portland's tax row covers every city in this metro, so this public fee is shown all-in with estimated food, service, and tax.

Ask on the tour
