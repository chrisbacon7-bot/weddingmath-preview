Redwood Deck at Hoyt Arboretum

Garden

4000 SW Fairview Blvd, Portland, OR 97221

All-in estimate

$6,409–$8,495

At 100 guests, Saturday. Published facility usage fee $330.00. Ceremony and reception for 40 standing or 25 seated, 8 a.m.–2 p.m. or 3–9 p.m. The combined meadow-and-deck fee is $533.50. Not ADA accessible. The 2027 Portland Parks & Recreation wedding brochure (valid for permits booked through 2027) lists this facility usage fee. A non-refundable application fee of $133.50 if you book more than 45 days ahead, $200.25 inside 45 days, or $266.75 inside 15 days, is extra and is not included. The brochure does not charge a different Saturday price, so both day columns use this fee.
