Wedding Meadow at Hoyt Arboretum

Garden

4000 SW Fairview Blvd, Portland, OR 97221

Published venue rental

$314

At 100 guests, Saturday. Published facility usage fee $313.50. Ceremony and reception, 8 a.m.–2 p.m. or 3–9 p.m. Booking this meadow with the Redwood Deck together is $533.50 instead of adding the two fees. Not ADA accessible. A $275 security deposit is extra. The 2027 Portland Parks & Recreation wedding brochure (valid for permits booked through 2027) lists this facility usage fee. A non-refundable application fee of $133.50 if you book more than 45 days ahead, $200.25 inside 45 days, or $266.75 inside 15 days, is extra and is not included. The brochure does not charge a different Saturday price, so both day columns use this fee.
