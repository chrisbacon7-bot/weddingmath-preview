Dawson Park Gazebo

Garden

N Stanton St and N Williams Ave, Portland, OR 97227

Published venue rental

$225

At 100 guests, Saturday. Published facility usage fee $224.50. Simple ceremony, two-hour limit, 12 chairs. The brochure's own example adds the $133.50 application fee to this $224.50 usage fee for a $358 total when booked more than 45 days ahead. That application fee is not included in the number on this card. ADA accessible. The 2027 Portland Parks & Recreation wedding brochure (valid for permits booked through 2027) lists this facility usage fee. A non-refundable application fee of $133.50 if you book more than 45 days ahead, $200.25 inside 45 days, or $266.75 inside 15 days, is extra and is not included. The brochure does not charge a different Saturday price, so both day columns use this fee.
