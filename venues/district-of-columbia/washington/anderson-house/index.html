Published venue rental

$18,000

At 100 guests, Saturday. Anderson House wedding page, Society of the Cincinnati. From April 1–June 30, August 1–October 31, and December, Saturday 6–11 p.m. is $18,000 and Friday is $17,000. From January 1–March 31, July, and November, Saturday is $17,000 and Friday or Sunday is $16,000. The figure here is the higher Saturday and the peak-season Friday. It does not include catering. Tax is not listed. Personal photo shoots at $800 or $1,200 are on a different page and are not used.

What's included

  • A Saturday wedding from 6 to 11 p.m. in peak season, up to 130 guests for ceremony, dinner, and dancing
  • A maximum nine-hour vendor block, with load-in at 3 or 4 p.m.
  • The site-rental overview says rates include security, vendor supervision, housekeeping, and docents, and that the house is wheelchair accessible

Not included

  • Catering and bar
  • Lower rates the page says to ask about for weddings without alcohol, without dancing, or with 50 or fewer guests
  • A smaller-event discount of $3,000 off Saturday night, which is not applied in the figure below

Ask on the tour

  3. Who is the caterer, and is there a food minimum on top of the rental?
  4. Does a wedding under 50 guests take $3,000 off the Saturday rate?
