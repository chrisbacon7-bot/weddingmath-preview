Heurich House Museum

HistoricGardenMuseum

1307 New Hampshire Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20036

Published Saturday range

$2,250–$4,500

At 100 guests, Saturday. Heurich House private biergarten page. Packages are $2,250 for up to 40 guests, $3,000 for 70, $3,750 for 100, and $4,500 for 150, each with drink tickets. They are Monday and Tuesday only, so this is not a Saturday rate. The historic house is not part of the rental. Outside food is extra. What's included

  • Three hours in the Castle Garden and Carriage House on a Monday or Tuesday, with 30 minutes of setup and museum staff
  • Drink tickets: 80, 140, 200, or 300 depending on the package
  • Outside food is allowed

Not included

  • The historic house, which the page says is not available for private events
  • A Saturday. Private reservations are Mondays and Tuesdays, 12–9 p.m., and not from Thanksgiving through March 1
  • Extra event time at $200 an hour and extra setup at $30

Ask on the tour

  3. Are the drink tickets beer and wine only?
  4. Can a ceremony happen in the garden on a Monday, or is the house required?
