Rockwood Manor

Historic

11001 MacArthur Boulevard, Potomac, Maryland 20854

Published Saturday range

$3,355–$5,250

At 100 guests, Saturday. Montgomery Parks Rockwood Manor social rate guide, checked October 3, 2026. The guide is headed for weddings, mitzvahs, celebrations, and holiday parties, for the manor house with grounds, 8 hours, up to 120 guests. In-season, April through October: Monday–Thursday $2,640, Friday, Sunday, or holiday $4,000, Saturday $5,250. Off-season, November through March: Monday–Thursday $1,660, Friday or Sunday $2,540, Saturday $3,355. The Saturday range is the off-season Saturday rate and the in-season Saturday rate. The off-day range runs from the off-season Monday–Thursday rate to the in-season Friday or Sunday rate. Amounts in parentheses are Montgomery County and Prince George’s County resident discounts and are not the base. An extra hour is $510 in season. A $750 initial payment and a $750 refundable security deposit are extra. Potomac is not in the Washington tax row used here, so this card stays a site fee.
