What's included

  • After-hours fee includes the space, indoor banquet tables and chairs if available, security, housekeeping, and event staff
  • Four-hour event, two-hour vendor load-in, and one-hour load-out
  • Events may be scheduled Thursday through Monday. The July 2025 grid does not split Saturday from other days

Not included

  • Catering, alcohol, linen, rental chairs, extra tables, a dance floor, and lighting
  • Outdoor tables and chairs. The museum arranges tenting exclusively
  • Gallery admission during public hours, and an after-hours gallery fee the sheet says will apply

Ask on the tour

  1. Which space is available Thursday–Monday: Rechter, Grand Lobby, Turner Courtyard, or a run-of-house package?
  2. What is the after-hours gallery fee for the first hour?
  3. Who may serve alcohol, and is there a minimum?
