What's included

  • The rental includes the contracted rooms, one hour to set up, 30 minutes to break down, event staff, and a set of tables and chairs
  • Free parking. Exhibit halls are available after hours or on Tuesday and Wednesday, when the center is closed
  • Weddings are listed among the celebrations the center hosts

Not included

  • The rental dollar amount. The page describes what a fee includes and does not print the fee
  • A damage deposit for exhibit-hall, after-6 p.m., alcohol, or youth events
  • Planetarium shows and science add-ons

Ask on the tour

  1. What is the Saturday after-hours fee for the West Galleries, and for the full center?
  2. Jack Wood Hall is the only daytime room on open days. Can a Saturday wedding use it before closing?
  3. What is the approved-caterer minimum, and who provides the ABC bartender?
