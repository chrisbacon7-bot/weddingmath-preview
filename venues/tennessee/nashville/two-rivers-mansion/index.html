Two Rivers Mansion

HistoricEstateGarden

3130 McGavock Pike, Nashville, TN 37214

Published venue rental

$3,700

At 100 guests, Saturday. Friends of Two Rivers: Saturday is $3,700 and Friday or Sunday is $3,000, for up to eight hours. Monday–Thursday is $2,000. A Monday–Thursday elopement is $1,200 for 3 hours and up to 30 guests, and that package includes an officiant, cake, champagne, wine, and mocktails. The Saturday figure does not include food or that elopement package. The page says event rentals are tax deductible; it does not state a sales-tax percent.

What's included

  • Up to eight hours in the 1859 mansion and lawn, with tables and chairs set inside
  • Dressing suite, catering kitchen, park benches for an outdoor ceremony, and free parking
  • The Friends of Two Rivers policies page says the mansion and grounds are handicap accessible, and wedding capacity is 125, with 85 seated indoors

Not included

  • A $500 refundable security deposit
  • Extra hours at $200
  • Outdoor chairs and a tent. House tables and chairs stay indoors, and dancing is outdoors
  • A TABC-permitted bartender from a caterer or bartending service. Guests may not bring their own alcohol

Ask on the tour
