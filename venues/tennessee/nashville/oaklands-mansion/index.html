What's included

  • Grand Italianate wedding, 10 a.m.–11 p.m.: gardens or grounds, Maney Hall, cottage as a changing space, verandas, patio, and a groom's room
  • That package and the Maney Hall reception include a one-hour rehearsal if available, 14 rounds, 125 chairs, 12 pub tables, and a portable bar
  • Maney Hall seats 110

Not included

  • Food. The page lists preferred caterers and bartenders and does not include a menu
  • A rehearsal dinner at $1,000, and self-guided mansion tours at $200
  • A tent, if the reception grows past the ballroom
  • The cottage alone, $100 an hour with a five-hour minimum, is a separate small rental

Ask on the tour

  1. Do these prices change on Saturday versus a weekday? The page does not say
  2. Is the $5,000 garden price ceremony-only, with no reception?
  3. Which preferred caterer is available, and what is the bar minimum?
