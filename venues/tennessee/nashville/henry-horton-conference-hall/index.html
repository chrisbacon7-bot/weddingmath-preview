Nashville · Chapel Hill

Henry Horton Conference Hall

Historic

4209 Nashville HWY, Chapel Hill, TN

Published venue rental

$1,250

At 100 guests, Saturday. Tennessee State Parks Henry Horton venues page, checked October 3, 2026. The Conference Hall is described as ideal for weddings and small conferences, with room for up to 200 guests. Available hours are 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. The rate is $1,250 and includes tables, chairs, setup, and cleanup. The page does not call the rate hourly and does not split Saturday from another day, so the same figure is used for an off day. Park catering is required. Taxes and fees are added upon reservation, and the percent is not printed. Chapel Hill is not in the tax table used here, so this card stays a site fee. The park is about 40 miles south of Nashville.
