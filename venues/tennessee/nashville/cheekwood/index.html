What's included

  • Weekday ceremony package: chairs for up to 30, a musician, bouquet and boutonniere, holding space, and an event supervisor
  • Available mid-March through early November, Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday evenings only

Not included

  • A Saturday wedding rental. The weddings page says to request venue pricing
  • Extra vendors. The ceremony PDF says no substitutions
