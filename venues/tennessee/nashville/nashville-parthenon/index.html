What's included

  • After public hours: art galleries, Naos Room, and Treasury
  • Indoor seated dinner for 250, or 500 for a standing event. The page also lists an outdoor plaza
  • A preferred-vendor list for catering, bar, and rentals. Tables and chairs are not described as included

Not included

  • The rental price. The page says to contact the venue for pricing and booking
  • Linens, place settings, and tents, which the page points to a rental company

Ask on the tour

  1. What is the Saturday after-hours fee, and what is Thursday or Sunday?
  2. Does the rental include the plaza and the galleries together?
  3. Is a caterer from the preferred list required?
