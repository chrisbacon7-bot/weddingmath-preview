What's included

  • The wedding page offers the Fels Planetarium and the Rooftop Deck for ceremonies
  • The 2024 events guide, still linked from that page, seats 300 for dinner in the Benjamin Franklin National Memorial and 200 in the planetarium
  • Seravezza Events, through Levy, is the exclusive caterer

Not included

  • A venue-rental dollar amount. The guide prices lighting add-ons and does not price the room
  • Those add-ons, including portable uplighting at $500, are not the rental

Ask on the tour
