How to read the wedding cost sources

If a dollar amount appears on a tool or a state page, it is in the tables here, or it is labeled as math from these tables. City studies, state studies, and estimated metros are labeled. The Knot and Zola stay in their own columns.

Common questions

Where do the wedding cost numbers come from?

The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study, average-cost article updated July 28, 2026, and the study readout. The Zola Wedding Cost Index, updated March 6, 2026. BEA Regional Price Parities, all items, 2024, released February 19, 2026.

Are The Knot and Zola averaged together?

No. The Knot average is $34,200 and the Zola average is $36,000. When both publish a local figure, both stay on the page.

Why is North Dakota missing a Zola range?

North Dakota was not in the Zola state table on the Wedding Cost Index page. The Knot state average is shown. No Zola range is filled in.
