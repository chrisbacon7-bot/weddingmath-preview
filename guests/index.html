    Common questions

    Does the guest list price each meal?

    No. A meal is a label you type. The per-guest figure is the planning total for the headcount who make the cut, divided by that headcount.

    Where is the list saved?

    In this browser. You can download a CSV or print the page. Nothing is sent to a vendor.
