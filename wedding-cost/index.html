How state wedding costs are chosen

Each state page starts with that state's published Knot average and Zola range. A city with its own study is listed separately. A metro without a wedding survey is an estimate: the state figure times the metro's BEA price level, divided by the state's. Washington, DC has city figures and no state row. North Dakota has no Zola range on the source page.

Common questions

How much does a wedding cost?

The Knot's 2026 average is $34,200 for couples married in 2025, $292 a guest, with a median of $24,000. Zola's 2026 average is $36,000. State pages show the published state figures.

Which state has its own wedding cost page?

All 50 states, plus Washington, DC. DC uses the published city figures because neither study prints a DC state row.

Do guest-count bands change the state average?

The Knot's national bands are $17,100, $27,200, and $43,300. They are a reference. At 117 guests, a Knot-based estimate matches the published average, so the bands are not used as a step multiplier.

Every figure is cited on the sources page.
