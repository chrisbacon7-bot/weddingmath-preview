About this page and common questions

Using the Washington, DC figures

At 117 guests, the planning figure matches The Knot's published Washington average of $42,000. Zola's $62,864 is the published 150-guest figure and stays beside it.

The District's all-items price level is 109.9, with the US at 100 (BEA, 2024). The metro's price level is 108.9.

Common questions

How much does a wedding cost in Washington, DC?

The Knot's 2026 city average is $42,000. Zola's 150-guest figure is $62,864. Neither study publishes a separate District of Columbia state average, so this page uses the city figures.

Is there a DC state average?

No. The Knot's state table and Zola's state table both skip the District of Columbia. The Washington city figures are the sourced numbers.

Does the Washington metro include other states?

Yes. The Washington-Arlington-Alexandria metro includes parts of Virginia, Maryland, and West Virginia. The published city figures are for Washington, DC, and this site uses them for that metro.
