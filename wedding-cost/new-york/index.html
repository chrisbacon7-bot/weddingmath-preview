Price a New York wedding

The Knot's 2026 average for a New York wedding is $49,000. That is $14,800 above the national Knot average of $34,200.

Zola's published New York budget range is $43,400–$65,100. Zola's national average is $36,000. The whole range sits above Zola's national average.

About this page and common questions

Using the New York figures

At 117 guests, the Knot-based planning figure matches the published New York average of $49,000. Other guest counts move food and a few other lines and leave the venue, photographer, and similar costs put. Zola's state range, when it exists, stays beside that figure.

New York's all-items price level is 107.9, with the US at 100, from BEA Regional Price Parities for 2024.

Common questions

How much does a wedding cost in New York?

The Knot's 2026 average for New York is $49,000. That is $14,800 above the national Knot average of $34,200. Zola's published New York budget range is $43,400–$65,100. It stays beside the Knot average of $49,000. The two are separate studies.

What is the wedding cost per guest in New York?

The Knot does not publish a separate New York per-guest rate. Dividing the state average of $49,000 by the national average guest count of 117 is $419. That division is labeled as math, not as its own published rate. The national published rate is $292.

Which cities in New York have their own wedding cost figures?

New York has a published city figure, listed above the state average. Other metros in the state are estimates.

What if our New York city has no wedding study?

The state average is the sourced starting point. A metro without its own study is estimated by multiplying the state figures by that metro's BEA price level divided by New York's price level of 107.9 (US = 100). The page labels those rows estimated.

Every figure is cited on the sources page.
