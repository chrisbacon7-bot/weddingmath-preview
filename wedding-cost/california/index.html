Price a California wedding

The Knot's 2026 average for a California wedding is $39,000. That is $4,800 above the national Knot average of $34,200.

Zola's published California budget range is $34,500–$51,700. Zola's national average is $36,000. The range covers both sides of Zola's national average.

About this page and common questions

Using the California figures

At 117 guests, the Knot-based planning figure matches the published California average of $39,000. Other guest counts move food and a few other lines and leave the venue, photographer, and similar costs put. Zola's state range, when it exists, stays beside that figure.

California's all-items price level is 110.7, with the US at 100, from BEA Regional Price Parities for 2024.

Common questions

How much does a wedding cost in California?

The Knot's 2026 average for California is $39,000. That is $4,800 above the national Knot average of $34,200. Zola's published California budget range is $34,500–$51,700. It stays beside the Knot average of $39,000. The two are separate studies.

What is the wedding cost per guest in California?

The Knot does not publish a separate California per-guest rate. Dividing the state average of $39,000 by the national average guest count of 117 is $333. That division is labeled as math, not as its own published rate. The national published rate is $292.

Which cities in California have their own wedding cost figures?

Los Angeles, San Diego, San Francisco have a published city figure, listed above the state average. Other metros in the state are estimates.

What if our California city has no wedding study?

The state average is the sourced starting point. A metro without its own study is estimated by multiplying the state figures by that metro's BEA price level divided by California's price level of 110.7 (US = 100). The page labels those rows estimated.

Every figure is cited on the sources page.
