Price a South Carolina wedding

The Knot's 2026 average for a South Carolina wedding is $35,000. That is $800 above the national Knot average of $34,200.

Zola's published South Carolina budget range is $25,900–$38,800. Zola's national average is $36,000. The range covers both sides of Zola's national average.

About this page and common questions

Using the South Carolina figures

At 117 guests, the Knot-based planning figure matches the published South Carolina average of $35,000. Other guest counts move food and a few other lines and leave the venue, photographer, and similar costs put. Zola's state range, when it exists, stays beside that figure.

South Carolina's all-items price level is 93.7, with the US at 100, from BEA Regional Price Parities for 2024.

Common questions

How much does a wedding cost in South Carolina?

The Knot's 2026 average for South Carolina is $35,000. That is $800 above the national Knot average of $34,200. Zola's published South Carolina budget range is $25,900–$38,800. It stays beside the Knot average of $35,000. The two are separate studies.

What is the wedding cost per guest in South Carolina?

The Knot does not publish a separate South Carolina per-guest rate. Dividing the state average of $35,000 by the national average guest count of 117 is $299. That division is labeled as math, not as its own published rate. The national published rate is $292.

Which cities in South Carolina have their own wedding cost figures?

No city in South Carolina has its own Knot or Zola wedding figure in these studies. Use the state average, or an estimated metro row if you are in one.

What if our South Carolina city has no wedding study?

The state average is the sourced starting point. A metro without its own study is estimated by multiplying the state figures by that metro's BEA price level divided by South Carolina's price level of 93.7 (US = 100). The page labels those rows estimated.

Every figure is cited on the sources page.
