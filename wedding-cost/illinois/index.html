Price an Illinois wedding

The Knot's 2026 average for a Illinois wedding is $38,000. That is $3,800 above the national Knot average of $34,200.

Zola's published Illinois budget range is $32,000–$48,000. Zola's national average is $36,000. The range covers both sides of Zola's national average.

About this page and common questions

Using the Illinois figures

At 117 guests, the Knot-based planning figure matches the published Illinois average of $38,000. Other guest counts move food and a few other lines and leave the venue, photographer, and similar costs put. Zola's state range, when it exists, stays beside that figure.

Illinois's all-items price level is 100.0, with the US at 100, from BEA Regional Price Parities for 2024.

Common questions

How much does a wedding cost in Illinois?

The Knot's 2026 average for Illinois is $38,000. That is $3,800 above the national Knot average of $34,200. Zola's published Illinois budget range is $32,000–$48,000. It stays beside the Knot average of $38,000. The two are separate studies.

What is the wedding cost per guest in Illinois?

The Knot does not publish a separate Illinois per-guest rate. Dividing the state average of $38,000 by the national average guest count of 117 is $325. That division is labeled as math, not as its own published rate. The national published rate is $292.

Which cities in Illinois have their own wedding cost figures?

Chicago has a published city figure, listed above the state average. Other metros in the state are estimates.

What if our Illinois city has no wedding study?

The state average is the sourced starting point. A metro without its own study is estimated by multiplying the state figures by that metro's BEA price level divided by Illinois's price level of 100.0 (US = 100). The page labels those rows estimated.
