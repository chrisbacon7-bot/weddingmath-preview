How Much Does a Wedding Cost in Colorado?

The Knot's 2026 average for a Colorado wedding is $31,000. That is $3,200 below the national Knot average of $34,200.

The Knot

$31,000

Zola

$27,000–$40,400

Published budget range. Updated March 6, 2026. Shown beside The Knot.

Zola's published Colorado budget range is $27,000–$40,400. Zola's national average is $36,000. The range covers both sides of Zola's national average.

Using the Colorado figures

At 117 guests, the Knot-based planning figure matches the published Colorado average of $31,000. Other guest counts move food and a few other lines and leave the venue, photographer, and similar costs put. Zola's state range, when it exists, stays beside that figure.

Colorado's all-items price level is 103.1, with the US at 100, from BEA Regional Price Parities for 2024.

Common questions

How much does a wedding cost in Colorado?

The Knot's 2026 average for Colorado is $31,000. That is $3,200 below the national Knot average of $34,200. Zola's published Colorado budget range is $27,000–$40,400. It stays beside the Knot average of $31,000. The two are separate studies.

What is the wedding cost per guest in Colorado?

The Knot does not publish a separate Colorado per-guest rate. Dividing the state average of $31,000 by the national average guest count of 117 is $265. That division is labeled as math, not as its own published rate. The national published rate is $292.

Which cities in Colorado have their own wedding cost figures?

Denver has a published city figure, listed above the state average. Other metros in the state are estimates.

What if our Colorado city has no wedding study?

The state average is the sourced starting point. A metro without its own study is estimated by multiplying the state figures by that metro's BEA price level divided by Colorado's price level of 103.1 (US = 100). The page labels those rows estimated.
