Price a Pennsylvania wedding

The Knot's 2026 average for a Pennsylvania wedding is $36,000. That is $1,800 above the national Knot average of $34,200.

Zola's published Pennsylvania budget range is $25,800–$38,700. Zola's national average is $36,000. The range covers both sides of Zola's national average.

About this page and common questions

Using the Pennsylvania figures

At 117 guests, the Knot-based planning figure matches the published Pennsylvania average of $36,000. Other guest counts move food and a few other lines and leave the venue, photographer, and similar costs put. Zola's state range, when it exists, stays beside that figure.

Pennsylvania's all-items price level is 97.6, with the US at 100, from BEA Regional Price Parities for 2024.

Common questions

How much does a wedding cost in Pennsylvania?

The Knot's 2026 average for Pennsylvania is $36,000. That is $1,800 above the national Knot average of $34,200. Zola's published Pennsylvania budget range is $25,800–$38,700. It stays beside the Knot average of $36,000. The two are separate studies.

What is the wedding cost per guest in Pennsylvania?

The Knot does not publish a separate Pennsylvania per-guest rate. Dividing the state average of $36,000 by the national average guest count of 117 is $308. That division is labeled as math, not as its own published rate. The national published rate is $292.

Which cities in Pennsylvania have their own wedding cost figures?

Philadelphia has a published city figure, listed above the state average. Other metros in the state are estimates.

What if our Pennsylvania city has no wedding study?

The state average is the sourced starting point. A metro without its own study is estimated by multiplying the state figures by that metro's BEA price level divided by Pennsylvania's price level of 97.6 (US = 100). The page labels those rows estimated.

Every figure is cited on the sources page.
