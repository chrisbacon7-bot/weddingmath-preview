Price a Louisiana wedding

The Knot's 2026 average for a Louisiana wedding is $34,000. That is $200 below the national Knot average of $34,200.

Zola's published Louisiana budget range is $23,800–$35,700. Zola's national average is $36,000. The whole range sits below Zola's national average.

About this page and common questions

Using the Louisiana figures

At 117 guests, the Knot-based planning figure matches the published Louisiana average of $34,000. Other guest counts move food and a few other lines and leave the venue, photographer, and similar costs put. Zola's state range, when it exists, stays beside that figure.

Louisiana's all-items price level is 88.2, with the US at 100, from BEA Regional Price Parities for 2024.

Common questions

How much does a wedding cost in Louisiana?

The Knot's 2026 average for Louisiana is $34,000. That is $200 below the national Knot average of $34,200. Zola's published Louisiana budget range is $23,800–$35,700. It stays beside the Knot average of $34,000. The two are separate studies.

What is the wedding cost per guest in Louisiana?

The Knot does not publish a separate Louisiana per-guest rate. Dividing the state average of $34,000 by the national average guest count of 117 is $291. That division is labeled as math, not as its own published rate. The national published rate is $292.

Which cities in Louisiana have their own wedding cost figures?

No city in Louisiana has its own Knot or Zola wedding figure in these studies. Use the state average, or an estimated metro row if you are in one.

What if our Louisiana city has no wedding study?

The state average is the sourced starting point. A metro without its own study is estimated by multiplying the state figures by that metro's BEA price level divided by Louisiana's price level of 88.2 (US = 100). The page labels those rows estimated.

Every figure is cited on the sources page.
