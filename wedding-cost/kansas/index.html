How Much Does a Wedding Cost in Kansas?

The Knot's 2026 average for a Kansas wedding is $20,000. That is $14,200 below the national Knot average of $34,200.

Published budget range. Updated March 6, 2026. Shown beside The Knot.

Zola's published Kansas budget range is $18,100–$27,100. Zola's national average is $36,000. The whole range sits below Zola's national average.

Price a Kansas wedding

Guest count

The Knot's national average guest count is 117. The $171 figure is $20,000 divided by 117. It is that division, not a separate published Kansas rate.

Using the Kansas figures

At 117 guests, the Knot-based planning figure matches the published Kansas average of $20,000. Other guest counts move food and a few other lines and leave the venue, photographer, and similar costs put. Zola's state range, when it exists, stays beside that figure.

Kansas's all-items price level is 90.1, with the US at 100, from BEA Regional Price Parities for 2024.

Common questions

How much does a wedding cost in Kansas?

The Knot's 2026 average for Kansas is $20,000. That is $14,200 below the national Knot average of $34,200. Zola's published Kansas budget range is $18,100–$27,100. It stays beside the Knot average of $20,000. The two are separate studies.

What is the wedding cost per guest in Kansas?

The Knot does not publish a separate Kansas per-guest rate. Dividing the state average of $20,000 by the national average guest count of 117 is $171. That division is labeled as math, not as its own published rate. The national published rate is $292.

Which cities in Kansas have their own wedding cost figures?

No city in Kansas has its own Knot or Zola wedding figure in these studies. Use the state average, or an estimated metro row if you are in one.

What if our Kansas city has no wedding study?

The state average is the sourced starting point. A metro without its own study is estimated by multiplying the state figures by that metro's BEA price level divided by Kansas's price level of 90.1 (US = 100). The page labels those rows estimated.
