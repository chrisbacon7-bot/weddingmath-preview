    What's a service charge?

    A percent added to the bill, often for staffing. Zola says it commonly runs 18–22%, and its venue guide mentions 18–25%. In Zola's survey, 57% of couples faced a mandatory venue service fee. A service charge is not automatically a tip. Read the contract before you add gratuity on top. "Plus-plus" means the menu price still needs the service charge and the tax.     What the wedding cost per guest includes

    The Knot's national average is $292 per guest, from the $34,200 average and 117 guests. A local all-in figure divides the whole wedding by the guest count. Cutting guests saves less than that all-in rate, because the venue, photographer, and similar costs stay.

    Common questions

    What is the average wedding cost per guest?

    The Knot's 2026 national figure is $292 per guest. Local pages divide a state or city total by the guest count and label that division. They are not extra published rates.

    How much do we save by cutting wedding guests?

    Less than the all-in cost per guest. Food, drinks, and a few other lines shrink. The venue, photographer, music, and similar costs stay. The calculator shows both numbers.

    What is a wedding service charge?

    A percent added to the bill, often for staffing. Zola says it commonly runs 18–22%, and its venue guide mentions 18–25%. A service charge is not automatically a tip. Plus-plus means the menu price still needs the service charge and the tax.

    What do hidden wedding costs average?

    Zola's spend survey put unexpected costs at $3,314 on average. That is a national survey figure, not a line on your quote. 57% of couples in that survey had a mandatory venue service fee.
