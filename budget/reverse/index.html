How Much Can We Afford for a Wedding

Add up what you have and what you can save. We'll turn that into a guest count for your city, using the local cost, not a national average.

    Fine-tune

    Only what someone has actually offered. Leave it blank if you're not sure.

    How much you can afford for a wedding

    Add what you have saved, what you can set aside each month, and only the family help someone has actually offered. The total is yours. The guest count is how many people that total covers at the local Knot-based cost.

    Nationally, The Knot's average is $34,200 and the median is $24,000. A local figure can sit far from either one. Where the local number is a range, the comfortable guest count covers the high end and the stretch count reaches the low end.

    Common questions

    How much can we afford for a wedding?

    Add savings, monthly savings times the months until the wedding, and family help that has been offered. That sum is the total. The tool then estimates how many guests it covers where you are getting married.

    Should we plan from the average or the median?

    The Knot's average is $34,200 and the median is $24,000. The median is the middle couple. The average is pulled up by expensive weddings. Your total is the one to plan from.

    What if our total is below the local estimate?

    The guest count falls, including below a typical wedding. You can also compare a shorter list on the cost-per-guest page. Some bills, such as the venue and the photographer, stay put when the list shrinks.
