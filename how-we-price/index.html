Pricing

How we price a venue

Every number on a venue page has a source and a date. Nothing is sent to the venue to get it.

Labels you will see

  • Published. The figure is on the venue's own website or PDF, or on the exclusive caterer's own price list when the venue names that caterer.
  • Public fee schedule. The figure is on a city, county, or park fee schedule.
  • Estimated. The figure is a study range or an official local tax rate applied to a line the venue did not price. It is not a quote.

Venue-verified and couple-reported are in the data model for later. They are not shown.

What “all-in” means

An all-in total needs four parts at your guest count, day, and season: the site fee, food or a catering estimate, a service charge, and tax. Each part is either published or labeled estimated. A missing part is left blank. It is not filled in as $0.

When the venue has not published food, the food line is The Knot's food figure for that place. The high end of the range adds The Knot's drinks line. The service charge, when the venue has not printed one, is Zola's 18–22% range. Those two studies are not averaged together.

Sales tax comes from a state or city revenue department, or from the percent the venue prints. A rental is taxed only when the venue's price list says so.

Freshness

A price older than 12 months is marked stale. The re-check script also flags source links that come back not found. A stale price stays on the page with the date. It is not quietly refreshed.

Prices change. Confirm the total with the venue before you book. Refundable deposits are listed apart from the total when a price list states them.

About this page and common questions

Common questions

Do you invent a tax rate?

No. Tax is the percent on the venue's price list, or a rate from a state or city revenue department. If neither is on file, the tax line stays blank.

What is an estimated line?

A study range or an official local rate applied where the venue did not print that part. The label says Estimated, and the source is on the line.

Every figure is cited on the sources page.
