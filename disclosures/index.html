How this site makes money

It doesn't, yet. The rules below are the ones the numbers already follow.

No account, no lead list

You can price a wedding without an email address. Nothing you type is sent to a vendor, a venue, or an advertiser. Budget totals stay in this browser unless you copy a link or download a CSV.

Estimates are not quotes

City and state figures come from The Knot's 2026 Real Weddings Study and Zola's 2026 Wedding Cost Index. Metros without their own wedding survey get a labeled estimate: the state figure, scaled by federal price levels. A venue contract can be higher or lower. The hidden-cost checker only adds the percents you type.

The studies are not blended

When The Knot and Zola both publish a figure, you see both. The planning number uses one stated method. It is never the average of the two.

Affiliates and featured listings

The shopping lists link to Amazon with rel="sponsored nofollow". If AMAZON_TAG in the site config is filled in, a purchase can earn a commission. The tag is empty in this version, so each link is a plain amazon.com/dp/ page with no tag. A commission does not change which products are listed, the order of a list, or any wedding total. A price is shown only as “about $X, checked Oct 3, 2026” after that product page was opened. A missing price means it was not confirmed. Commission never picks the number.

VacationMath

Honeymoon links go to VacationMath, a sister travel site. They use utm_source, utm_medium=referral, and utm_campaign=honeymoon so the handoff can be counted. They are not paid ads.

Analytics

No analytics tag is installed until a measurement id is added in the site config. Tool pages don't carry display ads.
